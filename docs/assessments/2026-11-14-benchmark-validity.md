Status: Proposed 2026-11-14 (architect ruling on owner direction).

Plan: `docs/plans/bench-environments.md`. ADR drafts: ADR-108 and ADR-109.

## Scope and verdict

VOID is not evidence of failure and not of success. Historical evidence cannot sustain an empirical
win or refusal unless its complete validity conjunction passes. Preserve descriptive facts, methods and independent
boundary reasons. Reopen unsupported empirical conclusions as UNTESTED, not as a refusal or a new win.
No model, benchmark or heavy gate was run for this audit. Tables describe committed evidence, not reconstructed
transcripts. A missing receipt is unknown, never proof that an operator did nothing.

BV-D1 requires exact product/model pins, per-arm environment preflight evidence, admitted concept counts, preregistered effective
trial counts and no relevant defect. Proposed age bound: thirty days at citation, with immediate staleness after
relevant changes. Native model-free readings need native invocation provenance, not a daemon connection.
Keep every failure flag. Primary precedence: VOID, STALE, UNFIT, UNDERPOWERED, VALID.
For historical rows below, `?` means an unestablished prerequisite. It is not an invented historical failure rate.
`none recorded` in the defect column is not a certificate of instrument correctness.
A historical PASS/STOP/CUT is quoted as its original verdict, never adopted as this audit's validity class.
Correction 1 and BX-40 remain preserved (`docs/design/bench-prep.md:36`, Scoring by assignment at line 371).
New WPF-4 preflight proves W0-W3/W6 independently before agent action. Only its failure voids the whole pair
on wiring grounds. W4 use and W5 acted-on are outcomes. Agent non-use never excludes a healthy assigned-arm cell.
For old readings, wiring means registration and reachability evidence rather than an agent's voluntary call.
Only assigned-arm estimates support headlines or rulings. A use-conditioned secondary is labelled
"biased toward fathom: selects on tool use". Other validity failures, including model aliases, still apply.
Reopened premises require fresh readings. ENV-6 may retain old VOID evidence for the audit, while ENV-C8 refuses
to import it as a re-test. Fresh free owners and named preregistration/spend rows are in the ENV plan.
