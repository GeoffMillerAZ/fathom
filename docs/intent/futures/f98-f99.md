# Future directions: F98 to F99

Records of the futures router, `../futures.md` (the confidence and horizon key, and every id with its member).

## F98. `cmd/fathom` split into packages

- Confidence: plausible (`docs/assessments/2026-11-04-standalone-capabilities.md`, 2026-11-04). Horizon: later. Evidence: 143,462 of 473,461 lines (30%) in one `package main`, across 1,184 files, importing 102 internal packages.
- Motivation: anything in the package can reach anything else, and its tests are the slowest in the suite. Verbs under `internal/cli/<verb>` with `main` as a thin table would give boundaries and faster selected tests.
- Do not build: a mass move while language or product lanes are open, or a move with no measured gain.
- Preserve: new verbs keep their logic in a package where that is free, as `internal/analysis/testinputs` does.
- Promote when: `go test ./cmd/fathom` is the top local test cost in a TQ-M7 reading, and the work queue has a quiet week.
- Drop if: test selection brings that cost under a minute on a typical change.

## F99. Further public doors

- Confidence: plausible (ADR-092, 2026-11-04). Horizon: later. Evidence: small closures measured in `docs/assessments/2026-11-04-standalone-capabilities.md`: route inventory, incident to code, agent command guards, multi-harness setup, budgets and shedding, the grading harness.
- Motivation: each could serve a tool that has no use for the rest of fathom.
- Do not build: any door without a consumer named in writing (P20).
- Preserve: keep these packages' closures small, and put new product imports behind a port where that is free.
- Promote when: a consumer is named in writing for one of them.
- Drop if: LL-M1 or G50's kill reads against the first door, or no consumer appears by 2028-01-01.
