## F86. Component corpora: per-component audit, plans, guard and review in a monorepo

- Confidence: plausible (ADR-074, 2026-10-17). Horizon: later. Evidence: the intent-practice-v2 monorepo research
  (research `wf_888f8f2d-57b`, review `wf_a6ee03dd-707`): fathom audits and plans only the repository root, so a
  component's own intent is invisible to `fathom intent audit` and `fathom plans`; platform-engineering's ids clash
  across its homes (F1 in 7, G1 in 5).
- Motivation: a monorepo whose components each carry their own intent gets the same audit, plan progress, generated
  guard entries and review per component that a single-scope repository gets at its root, with cross-scope citations in
  the kit's `<scope> <id>` form.
- Do not build: discovery of nested routers (a vendored kit's router would be read as a component).
- Preserve: components come only from the map rows of the monorepo's root repo.md (the kit's monorepo charter), never
  discovered; fathom's own repository stays single-scope.
- Promote when: the owner asks fathom to audit or plan a monorepo component, or a second monorepo adopter appears.
- Drop if: platform-engineering consolidates to one intent scope.

## F87. A dedupe view: a repeated result served as a pointer to the earlier one (readouts seam H7)

- Confidence: speculative (RO-M19, 2026-10-18). Horizon: later. Evidence: `docs/bench/readings/readouts/seam-dedupe-sizing.md`.
  Over 30 days, exact repeats of Bash and Read results and Read spans inside an earlier span of an unchanged file,
  since the last compaction, were 0.40% of Bash and Read bytes, against the 1% bar. RO-M0 had already read CUT on
  the lossy track.
- Motivation: an agent shown a result it already holds could be shown a pointer instead, if repeats ever become a
  real share of what agents read.
- Do not build: RO-47 (`dedupeView`) or the view channel it rides (RO-20, RO-M2) on this evidence.
- Promote when: a later `fathom readout replay` (its `dedupe` section) reads repeats at 1% or more of Bash and Read bytes over 30 days. The
  first step is then RO-M19's unrecorded half: one captured PostToolBatch input, showing what it carries and whether
  its output can replace a result.
- Drop if: Claude Code drops repeated results from context itself.
