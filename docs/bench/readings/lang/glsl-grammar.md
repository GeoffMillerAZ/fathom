The former unpatched-grammar refusal is superseded by the [2026-10-26 correction and remeasurement](../../../assessments/2026-10-26-glsl-registered-extensions.md). The committed [reading](glsl.json) measures all 968 unchanged canonical shaders: 39 ERROR-or-MISSING files (4.03%), zero faults, L1 good and L2 good. The obsolete LB-Q-glsl-rest refusal row is retired under G-R5. The controller owns proof of the main LB-Q-glsl row.

The pinned upstream release remains `tree-sitter-grammars/tree-sitter-glsl` v0.2.0, commit `e47b8b62b59d0e3529f1c31b03e025d6bd475044`. The in-tree patch mechanism applies the lock-listed GLSL and inherited-C patches before generation. The lock and PROVENANCE record the upstream archive and each patch digest. Two strict builds produce identical raw and gzip bytes.

The correction preserves macro precision identifiers and makes sized and unsized array-constructor callees consistent. Authored WASM, native and corpus tests prove these constructs. Statement-subtree checks on two pinned libretro trees provide a separate regression diagnostic. Neither holdout enters the canonical corpus. Full raw-file errors elsewhere do not disprove a clean precision statement.
