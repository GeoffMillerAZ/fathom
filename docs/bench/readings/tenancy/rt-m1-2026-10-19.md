## What it means

1. **The tenant clauses held.** Peak footprint stayed under the exit mark at every sample and under budget outside
   refuse windows; no refuse minute was self-caused; no exit rung and no unclean exit. On the question RT-M1 gates,
   whether fathom's own consumption is safe enough to add tables and background work, this week passed.
2. **The availability bar failed, and the host caused it.** Every refuse window on the three failing days was
   entered on `disk floor: swap volume free < 5 GiB` (4.2 to 5.0 GiB) while fathom was under its shed mark. On 10-13
   and 10-14 fathom held about 70 MB, so refusing freed nothing the host needed. The internal disk is the swap
   volume and the operator's agent waves build on it; on 2026-10-16 the operator raised the free-space floor a wave
   needs before it starts (15 GiB, 12 GiB for two workers), and no refuse minute has been logged since. That is a
   coincidence in time, not a measured cause.
3. **Shed is the larger defect.** The daemon spent 2,994 of 10,080 minutes (30%) in shed. On 10-12 to 10-14 its peak
   was 71 MB against a 3 GiB shed mark, so every shed minute those days came from host memory pressure `warn`. At
   shed the cache registry trims to half its budget and admits one rebuild at a time (`internal/memo`), so a host
   at `warn` for a third of the day evicts the call graph a hook-path call then rebuilds in 8 to 19 s (O1014).
   RT-M13 pre-registers at most 2% of a week in shed, else the host triggers are retuned; this log is 15 times that.
   RT-M13 has no verdict code and no registered window, so this is evidence for its retune, not its reading.
