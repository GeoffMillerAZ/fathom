# Adopter feedback triage, 2026-10-20: what 209 reports from seven repositories say

## Verdict

**What adopters hit most is fathom not answering.** 53 of the 209 reports (25%) are a graph-backed call or a literal search abandoned at the 60 s tree deadline with nothing served (T01, T02). Another 13 are the daemon itself down or restarting mid-session (T17). On the fleet daemon's own call log the failure is common, not anecdotal. `symbol_context` was abandoned on 25% of agent calls on fathom and 28% on app-kit, and `review_diff` on 38% on app-kit. The cause adopters could not see: every ingest and every restart evicts a service's graph (O1446, beside the budget cause O1014). On a repository that commits often, an agent's call nearly always pays a cold build, and the build is as long as the deadline.

**Second is answers that are stale or falsely empty and do not say so** (T05, T06, T07, T03, 27 reports). The index lags the tree after an edit. The notice that says so travels outside the part of the tool result an agent reads (O1449). symbol_context pairs indexed line numbers with current file text (O1450), answers an unresolved name with `def: null` and empty lists (O1451), and search treats a directory `path` as a glob that matches nothing (O1448).

**What is fixed:** 81 reports trace to causes that are fixed on main or working as designed. Another 20 are unconfirmed. For 12 setup reports filed 07-29 to 09-18 (T22) no fathom record turned up. 8 September reports (6 in T19, 1 each in T20 and T13) were not re-probed. The daemon serves binary `909ea5c2ecff`, built from main's head on 2026-10-20 (`/statusz`), and every adopting repository is served by that one daemon, so each of those fixes reaches every adopter today. The latest are the false "served rule ignored" line (O1434, 15 reports, fixed at 564df17cc) and the restart loop that started the daemon 307 times on 2026-10-18 (O1233 and O1363, 13 reports). Of the setup and doctor friction of July to September (28 reports, T22), 16 match a fix recorded in fathom's legacy log or ADR-038.

**What is still open, and why:** 104 reports in 13 themes had no fathom record. Each is now filed as one observation, O1446 to O1458, with its root cause. The largest (O1446) is blocked in its full form by the table and cache freeze until RT-M1 reads PASS, because keeping graphs across an ingest is an eviction-policy change. Its partial-answer half is not blocked. O1447, O1448, O1450, O1451, O1452, O1453 and O1455 are small changes inside the freeze, and none is built in this wave (no code change by the brief). O1458 (an O887 sibling) is not yet reproduced.

**The loop gap:** the adopters' journals still read open for 201 of 209 entries, including every entry whose cause is fixed here. Nothing carries a fathom fix back to the entry that reported it.

## Method

- **The index.** A classified index of the 209 adopter entries (journal, id, filed date, kind, surface, severity, misled, theme, a one-line summary). It is a local file and is not committed. Its theme was the starting cut. Entries were regrouped by shared cause: for example, search deadline reports were split from graph deadline reports, `def: null` misses from stale-index misses, and verify name collisions from verify's other false failures. 25 themes resulted, T01 to T25.
- **What was read.** Every entry's index row. The full text of 41 entries across the themes, read-only from the adopters' journals. Fathom's per-file journal (612 entries, title and status) and the legacy log (201 entries), searched by each theme's cause. Fathom's code at 909ea5c2e, at the sites each observation cites. The fleet daemon's call log: agent-origin outcome rows from 2026-07-07 to 2026-10-20, per tool and service, with latency and queue time. The daemon's stderr: start and drift-refresh counts per day.
- **Probes** (read-only calls to the fleet daemon on 2026-10-20, a known-good case beside each failure): symbol_context cold and warm on fathom and app-kit, and on agentic-kit with three key forms and a nonexistent name; search cold and warm, with a directory path, a trailing-slash path and a glob path, beside direct `rg` in the same tree; root obligations on app-kit; verify_plan with `plan_path` on adopter repository A's plan; pack_context with a sentence; find_symbol with an unknown argument, and on a grouped constant and an embed variable. The reads made the daemon queue its own drift refresh of app-kit and agentic-kit. No ingest was requested.
- **What was not done.** Not every entry was read in full. In T22, 12 of the 28 entries were left unconfirmed, with no record found and no probe. No harness sandbox session was run (O1457 was reproduced with macOS `sandbox-exec` instead), no daemon was profiled (O1446 and O1447 rest on cold/warm timings and the code), and agentic-kit O4 was not reproduced (O1458). No adopter journal was edited, and no fix was built (the brief's no-code-change rule).
