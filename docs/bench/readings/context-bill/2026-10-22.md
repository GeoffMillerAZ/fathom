# Context bill: bytes injected into agent sessions (2026-10-22)

CB-M1 bill: 2934124 bytes over 6.5 days

## Verdict

Window is 6.5 days, not 14: the hook log only holds rows from 2026-10-16T16:18Z to 2026-10-22T05:04Z (hooks.jsonl.1 starts there). Daemon call log also read from that start for comparability.

Unprompted hook injection totals 2.93 MB (~734k tokens), 40 identified sessions. Biggest channel: PostToolUse edit-time obligations, 1.49 MB, 51% of hook bytes, 1,590 injections at ~1 KB median. Next: subagent-start (18%) and cwd-changed (17.5%), both unnamed in the brief and bigger than the session primer (4%).
Per session: median 965 B (~240 tokens, primer only), mean 73 KB (~18k tokens), p95 896 KB. Two sessions hold 87% of bytes (1.66 MB and 0.90 MB): edit-heavy waves. Typical session is cheap, wave sessions are the bill.
Agent-requested MCP results dwarf the hooks: ~12.3 MB (~3.1M tokens) in the same window excluding verify_plan, ingest and review_diff, but those are pulls the agent chose, and the log has no session id, so not comparable per session.
Biggest lever: post-edit obligations (cap or dedupe per session; 43% of post-edit answers emit, pre-edit emits 0.07%). Second: subagent-start and cwd-changed, which re-inject each time (cwd-changed has 551 emits for ~2 sessions, ~935 B each).

## Method and data paths

- Hook log: $FATHOM_HOME/hooks.jsonl and hooks.jsonl.1; summed `meta.bytes` per (channel, kind, phase) for rows with a bytes field, window >= 2026-10-08 (data begins 2026-10-16). Emit rates from `meta.emitted` on edit-hook answered rows. Sessions from `meta.session`.
- Call log: <daemon-state>/logs/mcp-calls.jsonl, `response_bytes` per tool, filtered by `origin` and `caller.test`, from 2026-10-16T16:18Z.
- Instrument spec: cmd/fathom/payloadcost.go (payload-cost beats), cmd/fathom/research_report.go.
- Script: <scratch>/cb.py (inline python, no writes beyond <scratch>). Nothing touched the daemon or store.

Source: controller’s read-only, $0 hook-log reading dated 2026-10-22. Source paths are anonymized under ADR-011, and one silence description uses current help wording; counts and method are unchanged.

## CB-3: SubagentStart content reading

CB-3 measurement: generated `sample` service with 17 symbols, no discoveries, worktree or outbox: before 580 bytes; after 580 bytes. No cut.

Measured by `TestContextBillSubagentMeasurement` against `subagentPrimer`.
Both calls retain identity and the opening moves, once for each fresh subagent.
The current `SubagentStart` codec carries the **parent's** session id and the
child's agent id; it carries no proof that the child received its own
SessionStart or project instructions (`internal/harness/codec.go`,
`cmd/fathom/sessionhook_events.go`). The routing text overlaps the stanza,
but deleting it would remove needed guidance for children that never saw that
stanza. Parent discoveries, worktree advice and the child's map id are distinct
facts. Retain them and the existing 600/1200-byte caps. This is a deterministic
content measurement, not a seven-day savings result.

## CB-M2 protocol fixed before rollout

After seven days of actual use, re-read the hook log by session with the CB-M1
method. Exclude `hook-dedupe/suppressed` from injection totals (`bytes=0`), and
sum its `suppressed_bytes` separately by channel and session. Compare edit-heavy
sessions with CB-M1's per-session distribution: p95 896458 B and maximum
1657805 B; the two largest sessions were approximately 0.90 MB and 1.66 MB
and held 87% of baseline bytes. Require at least 40% lower hook bytes
per edit-heavy session. Check first-touch payloads against the non-deduped
rendering, and verify errors fail open. Tests cover mechanics; no field verdict
is available yet. Record the evidence and `CB-M2 verdict: PASS` or `FAIL` in
`after-dedupe.md` only after that observation window.
