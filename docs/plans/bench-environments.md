# Benchmark environments: admission, adequacy and symmetric re-tests

Status: Proposed 2026-11-14 (architect ruling on owner direction). Grammar v2.
Design: `docs/design/bench-environments.md` (BV-D1 to BV-D2, BE-D1 to BE-D9).
Records: ADR-108 and ADR-109 drafts. Reserved prefix: ENV. Every row is open.

These are specifications, not delivered commands or readings. Proof commands below are future acceptance commands.
A row is at most one lane-day. An admission can finish with a witnessed refusal. It cannot finish by waiving a bar.
Implementation lanes begin only after the architect freezes ENV-1's contracts and failing examples.
WPF receipt consumption waits on ADR-110 and wiring-proof.md#WPF-4, the common receipt gate.
All reading paths below are future artifacts. No existing reading or plan is duplicated or modified.

Shared admission bar: full pin/licence receipts, preserved C1 and C3 to C7, BE-D4 scale counts, independent concept
witnesses at BE-D2 minima and passing coverage cells. `ADMITTED` requires every prerequisite. `REFUSED` records
which failed or unknown prerequisite blocked it. Existing refused pins remain refused until an upstream repair or a
bounded preparation fix is witnessed. Receipt-backed subjects default to development. Holdout candidates need new,
unread identities. Public candidate size and licence unknowns are never filled from memory.

Shared reading bar: BV-D1 VALID before any effect verdict. Paired cells meet BEN-6's preregistered n and stopping
rule. If not, publish VOID, STALE, UNFIT or UNDERPOWERED, never a win or a feature kill. Each re-test runs a fresh measurement or waits on its explicitly named open measurement owner. No reading lane repeats paid cells already
owned by BEN, FTU, HZ, RL or CHG. Approval and BE-D8's paid gates apply to all model trials.
Each proof ends with `fathom verify --service fathom --plan docs/plans/bench-environments.md` before claiming done.

Correction 1 and BX-40 are preserved (`docs/design/bench-prep.md:36` and Scoring by assignment at line 371).
Only W0-W3/W6 scripted preflight, performed before agent action, gates wiring validity for the whole pair.
W4/W5 are outcomes. Keep non-use cells assigned to their arm. Only assigned-arm estimates support a headline or
ruling. A use-conditioned secondary is labelled biased toward fathom and never supports either.
ENV-6 retains legacy VOID evidence only as audit history. ENV-C8 refuses it as a fresh re-test input.
All commands and function names introduced below remain proposed contracts until implemented and verified.
