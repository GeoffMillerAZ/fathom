# Agentic decisions: stage-gated experiments and the `fathom ask` spine (EXP-, AQ-)

Design: `docs/design/decision-models.md` (sections 6 and 7). Decision: ADR-073 (decisions 9 and 10). Goal: G42.
Grammar v2 (`docs/plans/README.md`); a row flips to `[x]` only when `fathom verify --service fathom --plan
docs/plans/agentic-decisions.md` proves it. Beside `docs/plans/judged-predicates.md` (JP-: J0 and the deferred
benches) and `docs/plans/decision-port.md` (DP-, KS-).

The unknown that decides everything is whether a fast decision model reads code well; nobody has measured it (Q31).
These experiments grade a model against truth fathom computes, not one provider against another: calibrating Jev
against OpenAI is deferred (owner, 2026-10-16). Every call goes through `decide.Open` (provider `typesafe`, `transport
= "openrouter"`, the key through `api_key_file` only, never printed), never a vendor SDK, so a result carries to any
adapter. Budget (owner, 2026-10-16): up to $10 in all for Jev on OpenRouter; stage 0 at most $2; stage 1 at most $8
and only when EXP-M4 opens it; stage 2 needs the owner's separate approval; OpenAI Decisions spend is not covered and
is asked first. Each reading records its spend from the provider's reported usage, else estimated and labelled. Bars
are fixed here and in EXP-1 before any call (K19). Symbols are provisional until grounded with `find_symbol` at row
start.
