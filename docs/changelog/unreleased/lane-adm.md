### Added

- `fathom ingest` stops on SIGINT or SIGTERM by name: stderr says `error: ingest cancelled (SIGINT)` with how far the ingest got and what the store holds (read from its `ingest_state` after the stop: committed, `in_progress` after a write, or as it was), `--json` answers `outcome: "cancelled"` with `signal` and, when the ingest had not committed, `counts_partial: true`; no step after the one the signal stopped runs, and a second signal takes the default action at once. The exit is 130 (SIGINT) or 143 (SIGTERM): a shell's `$?` reads the same as before, but a Go parent's `ExitCode()` now reads 130 or 143 where the process used to die by the signal (-1), and a shell loop over `fathom ingest` now continues after Ctrl-C instead of stopping with it. A cancel before the first write puts the previous `ingest_state` row back, now also when it lands in discovery or before the ingest starts (RT-109).
- An ingest by `fathom ingest`, serve-http or serve-mcp beside another live process's uncommitted ingest of the same service in the same store is refused by name: `fathom ingest` exits 1 with `outcome: "busy"`, `owner_pid` and `owner_started_at` and names the row (the owner is still running or stopped partway) and the remedy; the daemon's `ingest` tool answers it class-scoped in the admission wire format; and auto-refresh defers a drift beside it (`refresh: deferred (another process is ingesting <svc>: pid N since T)`) instead of queueing a second ingest. Known limits: `fathom watch`, `ingest-diff` and `platform-ingest` do not check yet, two ingests that start in the same instant can both pass, and a long-lived owner's stopped ingest refuses until it re-ingests or restarts.
