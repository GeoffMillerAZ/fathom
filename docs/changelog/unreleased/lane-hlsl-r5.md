### Added

- HLSL (`.hlsl`, `.hlsli`, `.fxh`, and `.fx` files with shader structure: a `cbuffer` block, an `SV_` semantic, a register binding or a technique) extraction on tree-sitter-hlsl (the head of upstream pull request 82): functions, structs, cbuffers and tbuffers, typedefs, file-scope variables and macros, the calls a definition makes (intrinsics and member calls included; vector constructors, attributes and register clauses are not calls) and `#include` imports. A cbuffer header's register clause, a file-scope binding macro with no semicolon and a statement head split across `#if`/`#else` are read through an equal-length pre-parse step; 18 of the 644 calibration files still hold a parse error. The committed reading claims no level: its metrics grade L1 excellent and L2 good against DXC's AST (tier B) on the 541 files DXC reads, L3 is not met, and this machine's throughput (0.79 MB/s per CPU-second, under the 1 MB/s floor) awaits the integrator's re-measure. These extensions are no longer listed as unsupported languages.
