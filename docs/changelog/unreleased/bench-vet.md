### Added

- `scenariobench vet [-manifests <dir>] [-subjects-dir <dir>] [-work-dir <dir>] [-c6 <dir>] [-self-url <url>] [-corpus-dir <dir>] [-out <dir>] [name...]` vets each `calibration/bench.d` subject against C1 to C7 and writes `workspace/bench/vet/<name>.json`: each criterion's pass, fail or pending status with its evidence, sorted, and the suite runs' timings. It reads operator-prepared full clones and never clones or touches the network. C1 reads the top-level licence at the pinned sha; C2 counts the primary language's tracked lines at the sha, tests included (5,000 to 150,000); C3 runs one warm-up and `flake_runs` timed trials in docker with `--network none --cpus 4` as a non-root user against a prepared work directory (every run green, the longest at most 900 s) and reports any declared exclusions; C4 stays pending on the L0 reading; C5 mines Go fix commits (30, 15 after `fresh_after`; pending without a cutoff or for other languages); C6 reads a recount output (pending when missing); C7 refuses the checkout's own repository, a calibration-corpus repository and a third subject from one organization. Any failure refuses the subject; any pending criterion keeps it pending, never kept.
