# Advice delivery

Decision: [ADR-080](../../ADR.md#adr-080-advice-earns-its-tokens-readiness-imperative-raw-fallback-context-identity-and-measured-delivery-2026-10-22), accepted by the owner-architect, 2026-10-22.
Plan: `docs/plans/advice-delivery.md` (AD-). Boundary work: `docs/plans/boundary-enforcement.md` (BE-).

## Problem

The scrubbed 2026-10-22 [baseline reading](../bench/readings/advice-delivery/2026-10-22-baseline.md) measurements report about 113k injected tokens/day over 6.5 days without measured benefit. That is exposure without an outcome instrument, not proof that all channels have zero effect.

The post-edit hand sample has 12 relevant rules of 211 (5.7%); 31 of 40 injections have none. Eligible-rule counts are p50 97 and p90 618. October's full rule population includes 1,473 raw rows of 4,208 (35%). The separate 14-day staleness census has 1,804 raw rows of 5,197 serves (35%), with 17% of raw citations pointing into a different section. Layers last compiled on 2026-09-25 never compiled ADR-061 through ADR-079. The 10-16+ replay baseline is 603 injections, 175,590 payload tokens estimated as chars/4, 35.9% raw rows and 40.3% in-context repeats; preserve this population separately from the hand sample and the 14-day census.

The map line reached 0 of 1,841 subagent payloads. Clone-root refusal explains 91 of 121 failed search calls, not all clone exploration. The loose exploration metric's median 44 versus 44 becomes 21 versus 22 under the primary-verb classifier; neither uncontrolled comparison proves causality. A command wrapper hid 216 of 260 failing test runs (83%), and opt-in seams had zero log rows. Fixing wiring precedes judging their usefulness.

## Mechanism

1. **d1, readiness (AD-1 through AD-4).** Every harness Capability with Vehicle `hook-inject` names a cheap readiness predicate using information it already obtains. Keep three states distinct: off by choice stays silent; on but not ready stays silent and logs `skipped:not-ready:<reason>`; on but unused stays enabled and appears in doctor and a weekly reading. Health stamps support a single session-start inactive-channels line within I12. Doctor finishes within its existing hook budget and shows state, tokens/day and uptake; it flags double user/project registration. `fathom capabilities` lists opt-in seams. No extra readiness round trip or new background probe is allowed.
2. **d2, imperative or silent (AD-5).** Deterministically extract imperative lines from a raw section body or its ADR Decision lines. Serve the first two extracted lines in section order within the token cap; zero lines means silent. An imperative is a sentence or bullet containing must, never, always, do not, don't, shall, or required to as whole words; "only" counts only in the modal forms `only <verb>` at clause start or "may only"/"must only". ADR Decision lines are text under `**Decision**`/`**Decisions**` (including the existing period-suffixed labels) up to the next bold label; for numbered decisions take the first sentence of each item, first two items. Never serve only a heading. ADR-080 d2 widens ADR-077 d3 for both raw and compiled rows: Proposed, Rejected and Deferred records, files under `docs/plans/archive/**`, and checked `[x]` plan rows are not served; fully superseded sections stay withheld, and partly superseded sections stay served with their tag. Carry v0.17.0 citation status and re-find the heading in the live file at serve time. Name the count of uncompiled governing sections in doctor and the readiness line. ADR-037's guarantee is coverage visibility, not unconditional raw text.
3. **d3, context identity (AD-6 through AD-8).** Suppress a rule already delivered in this context and render `(k already shown)`. CB-1 still suppresses an unchanged whole payload, per-rule dedupe removes already-shown rules from a changed payload, and both keep CB-1's backstops (re-show after 30 minutes or 10 suppressions), compaction reset and `[hooks] dedupe = false` switch. Claude Code keys session plus `agent_id`, resetting on compaction in the CB-1 shape. Codex keys session plus transcript path when available and resets on PreCompact. A harness unable to establish a fresh context keeps full injections and doctor says why. Key memos by agent identity, never ask a subagent for a user verdict, and reserve “served rule ignored” for a checkable rule whose compliance was checked. Parser table tests cover sed `-e`, `-f`, `--expression` arguments, foreign `host:path` destinations, perl/ruby in-place writes, and quotes inside `$(...)`.
4. **d4, the indexed service across checkouts (AD-11 through AD-13).** Identity is the same origin URL and root commit, or the same git common directory. Normalize origin URLs by parsing scheme, user, host and path: ignore transport scheme and user for repository identity (ssh and https are equal), lowercase the host, preserve path case, and ignore trailing .git and trailing slash; scp-style ssh uses the same host/path key. Resolve relative local-path origins against their repository root; a local-path origin matches only a same-root-commit service. When two indexed services match, prefer same git common dir, then same root commit, else unknown. If the best rank still ties, return unknown. Serve base-index answers with `clone at <head>, index at <head>`; new or changed clone files are unknown, never absent. The subagent brief contains only the parent's own lookups, excluding hook-origin calls, junk and instruction files the harness already loads. Hash ranges, expire the brief before the parent session expires, and never ship raw daemon errors. A PreToolUse hook on the spawn tool appends the live parent map excerpt to the prompt where the harness supports input rewriting; `fathom map show --brief` supplies the same facts for orchestrators elsewhere. Do not merely merge separate thread logs or deliver unrelated maps.
5. **d5, honest claims and readouts (AD-14 through AD-17).** d5 applies to the Stop hook's output only; `fathom verify`'s exit codes and flags are unchanged. The message text changes to unknown instead of FAIL for unseen code, its format is unchanged: this is non-breaking. New or modified code unseen by the index, including an unindexed clone, reads `unknown (<reason>)`, never FAIL. Stop emits the failing-claim set once per digest change per session. Fix the unknown-base test delta and compiler-header recognition before activation. Add `observe` to every `[hooks]` seam while preserving `true` as on. A seam that cannot know, such as an unindexed root or a piped run without a verdict, stays quiet rather than inventing a negative.
6. **d6, deterministic sampling (AD-20).** Advisory choices use `hash(salt, session, target, ordinal) mod N`, logged as fired, sampled-out or held-out; first-touch advisory delivery can precede sampling, and holdouts key (session, rule). Never sample blocking guards, gates, verify, the readiness flag or failure-triggered lines. Reject that configuration in the schema. No clock or global random is a sampling input.
7. **d7, wrappers (AD-18 and AD-23).** Operator declarations describe prefix wrappers and the argument containing an embedded script. One shell lexer exposes effective commands to test-run, readouts, gate preflight, bash-write, discovery and guards. `rtk` is transparent; readouts exclude RTK-covered families and the pairing guide names exclusions. Do not hard-code a private command wrapper or build lossy compression inside fathom.
8. **d8, measure first (AD-9, AD-10, AD-19, AD-21 and AD-22).** Build $0 compliance and primary-verb exploration instruments before tuning. A serve-time precision gate is experimental and adopted only when replay increases relevance without losing any checkable relevant rule; it does not alter ADR-077 d2's fixed order. AD-10 is an operator-run compile of changed sections only; doctor names it as a next step when governing sections changed since the last compile. There is no model call in a hook and no background job. Operator opt-in and a spend cap remain required, with no spend authorized here. The repository seam trial waits for observe support and honest-unknown behavior, observes three days (set here), then uses rich/on under its pre-registered bars. No spend is authorized by this document.

## Failure and fail-open behavior

Infrastructure failure never blocks session, edit or Stop hooks (I9); readiness failure is logged, not converted into advice. Keep descriptor deadlines minus their existing reserve, without timeout retries (I53). Attention appears once within I12, without raising the cap. Unknown context or unreadable dedupe state falls back to full delivery so a first instruction cannot vanish. A failed clone identity match cannot assert file absence. A missing outcome cannot become a compliance violation, and a missing run verdict cannot become “new failure”.

Enforcement keeps its existing fail-open and blocking semantics and is never sampled. Observe records evidence but emits no advisory payload. Off stays off. No channel is automatically disabled because it is unused. Readiness uses no new table, column, cache or background job; context bookkeeping uses the existing hook-state mechanism. Product code performs no network fetch, and hook model calls remain forbidden.

## Generality

The mechanisms apply to any adopter with declared channels and any harness exposing the needed hook event. Context dedupe needs stable session/context identity and a reset event. Spawn-time map delivery needs a spawn PreToolUse event with input rewriting; otherwise an orchestrator explicitly inserts `map show --brief` output. Preserve byte-identical prompts where an orchestrator's resume contract requires them. Harnesses without a proven context boundary retain full injections. Missing wrapper declarations retain the ordinary parse path and report unknown where appropriate; no environment-specific command names are built in. A helper machine can resolve against a base index without claiming its changed files are indexed.

## Compatibility surface

This is an accepted design with unchecked implementation rows. It changes no runtime contract today. The implementation touches the listed compatibility surface in `docs/intent/scope.md`: CLI/configuration, hook stdin/stdout and telemetry. Each change to that surface must be reviewed with its required breaking fragment and migration note. Preserve `true` as on, fail-open behavior, deadlines, verify exit codes, plan grammar v2, store format and all payload caps. No MCP tool growth, schema table change, dispatch loop or governor policy is implied.

## Decision models

Two optional predicate audits could use the existing decision port: “is this compiled rule still faithful to its edited section?” and “does rule R apply to this diff?” Provider `none`, explicit opt-out, a missed deadline or an unqualified band keeps the deterministic extraction/freshness/scope result. Before enabling either site, declare a calibrated band under ADR-073; uncalibrated output stays labelled judged and cannot prune, block or assert a code fact (I62). Qualification must beat the deterministic baseline under EXP-M4's rule. EXP-M4 is CLOSED; this design does not reopen stopped sites or authorize spend. All readiness, identity, wrapper parsing, dedupe and hash sampling remain computed.

## Measurements

All new keep/kill thresholds in this design are set here unless attributed to a supplied study target or an existing reading; they are not empirical results. All readings are $0, state their input population and method before verdict, and record missing evidence as incomplete, never a passing zero. The historical scripts' abandoned proposals are not adopted merely because they appear in the evidence bundle.

- **AD-M1, after AD-5 through AD-8.** Replay the fixed 10-16+ targets and compare relevance and payload tokens with 603 injections / 175,590 estimated tokens, 35.9% raw and 40.3% repeats; hand-code a fresh 40-injection sample using the same relevance scheme. Keep only with relevance up, payload tokens down and zero checkable relevant rules lost; the study's target is at least 50% of emitted injections bearing on the edit. In the baseline, 9 of 40 is the full sample; 9 of 24 is the subset after removing mechanisms already fixed at HEAD. The full sample also has 12 of 211 relevant rules and 31 of 40 irrelevant injections; keep those denominators distinct. AD-9 additionally needs its own frozen no-loss replay; AD-10 needs a separately approved opt-in/cap and a re-read of uncompiled coverage and citation drift before retention.
- **AD-M2, after AD-11 through AD-13.** Compare subagent payload bytes, stale ranges, delivered map facts, clone search refusal and primary-verb exploration before first edit. Keep with fewer payload bytes, stale-line share below 1% against the study's 9%, clone-root refusals from 91 to zero and overall search failure share below 10% against 46%. Compare strict exploration and tokens with three comparable prior waves, not the inflated loose classifier; failure to deliver usable parent facts or to resolve a known clone fails the relevant part. Historical workload concentration prevents a general causal claim.
- **AD-M3, after AD-15 through AD-19.** Observe for three days (set here) before rich/on; retain only with no false answer and rich acted-on rate at least 56.8% (set here) versus 46.8% baseline. - **AD-M4, after AD-20 through AD-22.** Publish the first (session, rule) holdout reading with each channel's pre-registered effect bar, sample assignments, eligible/fired/sampled-out/held-out counts, checkable compliance and primary-verb exploration. AD-23 keeps external-filter pairing; internal lossy compression reopens only at RO-M0's existing 4% keep bar for Bash bytes in classes passing the harm bar that pairing cannot cover.
