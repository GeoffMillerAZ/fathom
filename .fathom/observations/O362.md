### O362 — whole-service ingest cost grew superlinear between 9ea6da5f and 32afe369

- **Symptom:** the LB-M5 polyglot set (17 repositories, 6,524 files, 75,971,918 bytes) costs 1,287.7 G instructions to ingest whole at 32afe369, 1.92× the 670.1 G its subjects cost ingested one at a time; at 9ea6da5f (wave w6) the whole tree read 523.3 G against 551.6 G summed (docs/bench/readings/lang/footprint.md).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `/usr/bin/time -l fathom ingest --json --store <fresh sqlite> --service poly --path <tree>` and the same per subject (nested one level, no go.mod at a service root), fresh `FATHOM_HOME` and `FATHOM_CACHE_ROOT` each run.
  - **Expected:** ingest cost proportional to the bytes and files read, as at w6.
  - **Actual:** whole 1,287.7 G (two valid runs: 1,283.5 G, 1,287.7 G) against 670.1 G summed; the ingest report's counts (85,206 symbols, 22,318 file_resolved, 3,226 doc units) give no per-pass time.
  - **Repro:** footprint.md's 2026-10-01 LB-M5 section (base 56c18502, main 32afe369, head 5556eb84).
- **Root cause:** not established: no per-pass timing in `ingest --json` and no profile taken (budget of this item). Candidates are the passes whose work grows with the whole service rather than per file: the file-resolved and reference-edge passes (wave lc, LB-F74..LB-F78, their export and scope indexes over every file), the doc-link pass, and centrality.
- **Fix:** none yet; the next step is an in-process CPU profile of a whole-tree ingest at 32afe369 against one at 9ea6da5f, or per-pass timings in the ingest report.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** LB-M5 reads FAIL at 4.84× CPU per byte (1.5× bound); this component alone is past the bound, whatever any dispatch switch saves.
