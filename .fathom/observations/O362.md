### O362 — whole-service ingest cost grew superlinear between 9ea6da5f and 32afe369

- **Symptom:** the LB-M5 polyglot set (17 repositories, 6,524 files, 75,971,918 bytes) costs 1,287.7 G instructions to ingest whole at 32afe369, 1.92× the 670.1 G its subjects cost ingested one at a time; at 9ea6da5f (wave w6) the whole tree read 523.3 G against 551.6 G summed (docs/bench/readings/lang/footprint.md).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `/usr/bin/time -l fathom ingest --json --store <fresh sqlite> --service poly --path <tree>` and the same per subject (nested one level, no go.mod at a service root), fresh `FATHOM_HOME` and `FATHOM_CACHE_ROOT` each run.
  - **Expected:** ingest cost proportional to the bytes and files read, as at w6.
  - **Actual:** whole 1,287.7 G (two valid runs: 1,283.5 G, 1,287.7 G) against 670.1 G summed; the ingest report's counts (85,206 symbols, 22,318 file_resolved, 3,226 doc units) give no per-pass time.
  - **Repro:** footprint.md's 2026-10-01 LB-M5 section (base 56c18502, main 32afe369, head 5556eb84).
- **Root cause:** the KR-6 doc-link pass (f76febde, inside the window). Its store resolver resolved each distinct identifier-shaped span name with `ObservationsForSymbol`, whose `symbol IN (...) OR symbol GLOB '*.name'` cannot range `idx_observations_service_symbol`, so every name scanned every observation of the service: names times symbols. Whole: 3,226 doc units over 85,299 symbols; one subject sees only its own symbols, so the sum stays small. Measured (wave ic, docs/bench/readings/efficiency-wave/ingest-linear.md): `pass_times` put the `doc_links` sub-pass at 48.9 s CPU whole against 4.7 s summed (10.4x) and every other pass at or under its sum; an in-process profile put `observationsForSymbolRaw` at 42.4% of the whole run's samples. A second, smaller term in the same pass: `SyncDocLinksForUnit` read a unit's open links through the (service_id, target) index, scanning every link of the service per unit (units times links). The file-resolved and reference-edge passes (LB-F74..LB-F78) read 0.80x their sum and are not superlinear; centrality reads about 1.4x (O410).
- **Fix:** EW-48 (c8a99868) times every pass on the ingest report (`pass_times`, one `pass-times` line). EW-49 (44a1a626): the link pass reads the service's (symbol, source_ref, content_hash) once per pass (`store.SymbolHashes`) into an index that reproduces `resolveSymbolNow`'s rule and scoping. EW-50 (e9dc2e9a): the pass reads the service's open doc_links once (`store.DocLinkSync` through `docindex.LinkPass`). Stored rows byte-identical on libuv, okhttp, Polly and the whole tree; no ExtractorEpoch bump.
- **Status:** fixed (wave ic, branch ic-linear)
- **Impact:** whole against summed on the LB-M5 set: 1,419.4 G against 784.5 G instructions (1.81x) before, 704.2 G against 728.1 G (0.97x) after (EW-51 PASS). The whole tree's cost halved (-50.4%). On the merged tree (wave ic integration, 3d795e02, with O361's fix) whole over summed reads 0.93 (485.8 G against 519.7 G) and LB-M5 FAIL at 1.92x CPU per byte, against 4.84x on 2026-10-01: what is left grows with the bytes, not the service (docs/bench/readings/lang/footprint.md, 2026-10-02).
