### O381 — Rego names do not compare under bare matching: hyphenated package paths stay whole on the oracle's side, and the oracle attributed calls by a ref head's last segment

- **Symptom:** the first Rego reading read sym_precision .7961 and sym_recall .9188; on regal, 176 package definitions unmatched both ways
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect rego` against calibration/oracles/opa-parse (OPA 1.18.2), StyraInc/regal v2.2.3
  - **Expected:** `regal.rules.bugs.annotation-without-metadata` (fathom) matches the same name (oracle)
  - **Actual:** under `name_match: bare` fathom's name is cut to `annotation-without-metadata` (BareName) while the oracle's stays whole (oracleMatchName cuts only an identifier path); and opa-parse named a call's caller by the rule's last segment (`c`) while naming its definition `a.b.c`
  - **Repro:** the reading's `-unmatched` file, dialect_only and oracle_only
- **Root cause:** the harness's two name normalizations disagree on a dotted path with a non-identifier segment, and the oracle used two names for one rule
- **Fix:** the rego dialect compares names whole (`name_match: identity`), its calls store the callee's last segment as the oracle does, and opa-parse names a call's caller as its definition (calibration/oracles/opa-parse README, 2026-10-01)
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** L1 reads 1 / 1 on the two subjects; any language whose qualified names carry hyphens needs identity matching
