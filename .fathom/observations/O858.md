### O858 — JavaScript's module-level let, var and exports.x bindings lift express to L4 excellent but cost +21.5% store on it: stopped at the cost gate for an owner ruling

- **Symptom:** the product change O749 asked for (wave lf5, item tsjs: a module-level `let` or `var`, each name a destructuring one binds, and `exports.x =` / `module.exports.x =` a value become JSConst definitions, modelled on LB-F382's const patterns) passes its tests and goldens, but its cost gate (K26/K28) reads express's store over the +20% bar.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** scratch ingests (`fathom ingest --store`, one at a time under a shared ingest lock), baseline 9e21be2b (the last ruled baseline, K28) against the change, three alternating rounds per binary, at load averages of 22 to 42.
  - **Cost, medians of three:** express df0abc93 store 1,748,992 to 2,125,824 bytes (+21.5%; observations +558 rows, all JSConst, symbol_centrality +543, typed_edges +357, observation_calls +307, doc_links +65), wall 2.34 to 2.56 s (+9.4%), CPU 1.77 to 1.84 s (+4.0%); zod 1fb56a5c store -0.2%, CPU -1.3%; nest 9ff83d54 store +0.0% (identical bytes), CPU +0.6%. A first series without CPU timing read nest's wall +43.6% on identical stores: wall at this load is noise, CPU is the comparable figure.
  - **Gain, express under the same harness (lang-oracle at 753ca697 against a fresh scip-typescript 0.4.0 index, single pass):** def_mapping .2964 to 1 (759 of 759), typed_recall .3651 to .9931 (3,600 of 3,625): L4 unmet to excellent; L1, L3 unchanged; call_precision .2733 to .2447 (express is over W3's per-subject cap either way: 1,406 then 1,704 blind pairs of fathom's).
  - **Why the store grows:** express (df0abc93) writes no `const` at all, so every module binding (the require bindings of its ~90 test files included) is a new symbol with its centrality row and the references it now receives; the growth is the gain's own rows, and no pattern in the dialect could keep the gain and drop the rows.
- **Root cause:** K26's +20% store bar, applied per subject, against a var-only codebase.
- **Fix:** shipped under owner ruling M1 (10-11 05:40Z, K26 ACCEPT: express store +21.5% and wall +9.4% for def_mapping .2964 to 1 and typed_recall .3651 to .9931 on the canonical CommonJS framework; Node is on the client tool list). Re-landed in wave lf6, item tsjs, from the held branch (ab21da2c, the one commit off main) onto e7bbd99e as 7b17f6df, its tests green under -race; the cost gate re-read against e7bbd99e, median of three alternating scratch ingests under the wave's ingest lock at a load near 11.5: express store 1,835,008 to 2,252,800 bytes (+22.8%; main's baseline grew), wall 1.33 to 1.52 s (+14.3%), CPU 1.17 to 1.23 s (+5.1%); zod store +0.1% (CPU -1.4%), nest identical bytes (CPU +0.0%), inside the ruling's re-measure bar (store +23%, wall +15%). LB-F470 flipped by `verify --strict`. Epoch need: every .js, .jsx, .mjs and .cjs file (the integrator's bump).
- **Status:** closed (owner ruling M1, K26 ACCEPT; LB-F470)
- **Impact:** a var-style CommonJS codebase keeps reading its module bindings as missing (express def_mapping .30, typed_recall .37) until the ruling; with it, express reads def_mapping 1 and typed_recall .9931 under the same harness (lf5's measurement).
