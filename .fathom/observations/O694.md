### O694 — three Express example apps register routes through their own helpers: the reader's recall ceiling

- **Symptom:** the Express reader (LB-F328) reads expressjs/express `examples/` at route and bound recall .7632, precision 1.0 (58 of 76 routes, none invented), under the .90 bar; the 18 misses are all in three apps
- **Evidence:** `docs/bench/readings/routes/node.json` (route-oracle against the router stack walk, `calibration/oracles/express`): `examples/mvc` 0 of 9 (`lib/boot.js` reads the controllers directory with `fs.readdirSync` and derives `GET /<name>/:<name>_id` from an export named `show`), `examples/route-map` 0 of 5 (`app.map`, a helper the example defines, walks a nested object), `examples/resource` 1 of 5 (`app.resource('/users', User)`); the other 22 apps read 1.0 / 1.0
- **Root cause:** each helper computes the method and the path at runtime (`app[method](path, fn)`), which a reader of source cannot follow without evaluating the app; the express-regex reader before it missed the same routes (and read .6974 / .8154 with no bindable handler)
- **Fix:** none in the reader; LB-F328-rest records the ceiling. Trigger: a corpus of production Express apps under .90 for a shape a reader can see (a route table array looped into `router.use(r.path, r.route)` is the most common one not followed), or a same-file helper evaluated with its literal arguments
- **Status:** open (ceiling, measured)
- **Impact:** an app that registers through its own route DSL keeps those routes out of `route:` keys and service_ref joins; everything registered with `app.METHOD`, `router.route()` and `use()` is read
