### O1466 — symbol_context reads its definition snippet on two paths now, and a snippet check added to one misses the other

- **Symptom:** none yet. A check added to the warm path's snippet read (the O1450 freshness check is the pending one) does not reach the snippet a cold answer serves.
- **Evidence:** `registerSymbolContext` (internal/mcp/nav_tools.go) reads the snippet inline. `symbolContextColdAnswer` (internal/mcp/cold_graph.go, O1446's partial answer) reads its own with the same `readSourceRange` call and the same `snippetWithheld` check, when the snapshot is resident. Wave fix6 kept them apart so the O1446 change would not edit the lines the O1450 fix owns.
- **Root cause:** two copies of one read, made to avoid a merge conflict between two items of one wave.
- **Fix:** proposed, not built. When O1450 lands, move the snippet read and its checks into one helper both paths call, and give the cold path's answer the same freshness field.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a cold `symbol_context` answer could pair indexed line numbers with current file text after O1450 fixed that on the warm path.
