### O843 — A queue retarget leaves the dialect manifest's target_level behind, and the gate notices only in the extractor package

- **Symptom:** after ADR-067's queue edits (2ad46bd1) raised bash, jenkinsfile, lua and powershell to L3, `TestDialectManifests` failed (`dialect bash: target_level L2, queue says L3`), while the queue and tracker tests named in the brief stayed green.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `go test -race ./internal/extractor -run TestDialectManifests` at 2ad46bd1 (wave lf5, item html).
  - **Actual:** four failures, one per raised dialect; fixed by 3eda224d (each manifest's target_level to L3).
  - **Repro:** raise a dialect's queue target without its manifest, then run the extractor package.
- **Root cause:** the target level lives twice (the queue entry and the manifest), held equal by a test outside `internal/langbench`, where a triage's checks run.
- **Fix:** 3eda224d. A triage that raises a target edits the manifest in the same commit; the roadmap's section 10 named the queue only.
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** siblings that rebased onto 2ad46bd1 saw a red extractor package until 3eda224d (design18.sha now names it).
