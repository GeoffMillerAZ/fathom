### O908 — after M3, C's remaining pairs to a later definer are types and extern declarations, which internal linkage does not cover

- **Symptom:** c.json re-read under owner ruling M3 still counts 174 file-edge truth pairs drawn to a second or later definer (`scip_file_edges_every_definer`: libuv 17, curl 157), plus 33 on the libevent holdout.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** a scratch classifier over the rebuilt scip-clang indexes and their clang-ast-defs companions. It lists every symbol that two or more documents define and marks each definer's internal linkage. It was never committed.
  - **libuv:** the multiply-defined symbols with an external or no-linkage definer fall into two groups. The first is struct types that several test files declare alike: `thread_ctx#` in three, `worker_config#` in three, `pinger_t#`, `buf_s#` and their fields. The second is external definitions: `main` in run-tests.c and run-benchmarks.c, `executable_path`, and test-tcp-write-after-connect.c's non-static `loop`, `tcp_client` and `buf`.
  - **curl:** objects that a header declares `extern` and a .c defines, such as `Curl_cft_tcp` (cf-socket.h and cf-socket.c), the `Curl_protocol_*` handlers, `Curl_cfree` and the other allocators (curl_setup.h and easy.c). scip-clang marks both the declaration and the definition as a Definition.
- **Root cause:** a struct tag has no linkage (C11 6.2.2p6), so each unit's `struct thread_ctx` is its own type. Two units' types are only compatible (6.2.7), and scip-clang names them alike. An extern declaration with its definition is one external entity (6.2.2p2), so ruling 3 draws both files for it. A reference to a header's declaration and to the .c that defines the object is a true dependency in both cases. Neither shape is internal linkage, so M3 leaves both to ruling 3.
- **Fix:** none needed for the grade, because C's L3 reads excellent (file_edge_recall .5615). A truth rule for same-named types across units would be the owner's to make (K19).
- **Status:** open (recorded residual).
- **Impact:** small: 174 of 7,120 truth pairs. The types pairs inflate libuv's test-file reach slightly.
