### O401 — a dialect claimed by content outside classify.go is invisible to the harness until lang-oracle learns its sniff

- **Symptom:** the first ARM-template reading scored 0 files: every azuredeploy.json was skipped as "not this dialect's"
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect arm-templates` (LB-F146)
  - **Expected:** the harness measures a dialect on the files ingest dispatches to it
  - **Actual:** ownedSourceRefs asks fusion.ContentDialect, whose list of content sniffs is hard-coded in classify.go (CloudFormation, OpenAPI, …); ARM's sniff lives in armdispatch.go
  - **Repro:** measure arm-templates with langoracle.go's AppFrameworkContentDialect line removed
- **Root cause:** two places decide "which dialect owns this .json": resolveExtractor (ingest) and ContentDialect (harness)
- **Fix:** wave iac added fusion.AppFrameworkContentDialect and one fallback line in langoracle.go; one registry of content sniffs would close it
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** every new content-claimed dialect needs a harness edit or reads empty
