### O1236 — Until a service's first build is measured, a short-deadline caller is declined even for a fast snapshot

- **Symptom:** RT-191's rule falls back to `ExpectedCost` when no build of a (group, service) has completed. The snapshot group's `ExpectedCost` is 2 s, so a caller with a deadline under 1 s (the pre-edit hook's 500 ms `blast_radius`) is declined on the snapshot too, even when listing that service's symbols would take milliseconds. Before RT-191, that call built the snapshot and then abandoned the graph.
- **Evidence:** `TestVerifyPlanAnswersADeclinedBuildAsItsStagePartial` (internal/mcp): a 600 ms verify_plan on a one-symbol service whose snapshot was never built gets the declined partial. 19 serving call sites in internal/mcp load through `serviceSnapshot` or `serviceSymbols`.
- **Root cause:** `ExpectedCost` is the worst-case cold build on the largest service (the RT-74/75 measurements), and RT-191 uses it as the unmeasured duration for every service. The record is per (group, store and service), survives watermarks and is never dropped, so this lasts only until the first completed build: the first hook calls after a daemon start, and a service that only short-deadline hooks ever ask about.
- **Fix:** read at RT-M14's K2 (the answered share). If `declined` on `snapshot` dominates the misses just after a start, use a measured fallback, such as the duration of the service's last ingest or a per-group default below the worst-case graph cost. `ExpectedCost` stays the bound on keeping a flight.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** after a daemon start, short-deadline hook calls answer the declined partial until a call with no deadline, or a long one, builds the service once.
