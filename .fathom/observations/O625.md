### O625 — the Stage A static kill fires on kube-prometheus by its denominator, not by a wrong join

- **Symptom:** label_join_recall .583 under the .60 kill line (docs/bench/readings/telemetry/stage-a-static.json), so the PrometheusRule join (LB-F312) is withheld
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-routes -label-joins` on prometheus-operator/kube-prometheus v0.19.0 at f0757ff9, truth from `kubernetes-oracle -labeljoins-out` (ServiceMonitor jobLabel semantics, Prometheus parser v0.315.0)
  - **Expected:** a static join reaching .60 of every alert and recording expression
  - **Actual:** 235 expressions; all 137 whose job names a Service the repository declares joined right (precision 1.0, recall 1.0 there); 58 read control-plane jobs (`apiserver` 28, `kubelet` 17, `kube-scheduler` 11, `kube-controller-manager` 2) whose Services the cluster creates; 40 have no job or service matcher. .583 is the maximum any static join reaches on this subject
- **Root cause:** the reading's denominator counts platform expressions (census partition P) that no repository declares a Service for
- **Fix:** none in code: the owner decides whether partition P leaves the denominator (recall .774) or a runtime oracle (Prometheus targets and series, tier A dynamic) replaces the static one; LB-F312's trigger names both
- **Status:** open (owner decision)
- **Impact:** an alert on an adopter's own service would join right today; it stays unjoined until the decision
