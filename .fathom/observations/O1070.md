### O1070 — MCP search returned the literal text "d" for a hit at tests/test_requests.py:692 in psf/requests

- **Symptom:** a search hit at tests/test_requests.py:692 (psf/requests 611c6162) rendered its text as `d` — the first rune of `def get_netrc_auth_mock(url):`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** MCP search
  - **Expected:** the matched line, cut around the match when it is long
  - **Actual:** a one-rune text, the match itself cut away
  - **Repro:** internal/mcp/search_hittext_test.go (a long test path, a long enclosing method, the test flag)
- **Root cause:** renderSearchHit fit each hit to its 120-byte budget by shrinking Text one rune at a time while every receipt field stayed exact, with no floor; a long receipt left the text its first rune.
- **Fix:** the budget never cuts the text below the match (plus its cut markers) or 24 runes; a line longer than the render bound is windowed on the match, "…" on each cut side.
- **Status:** fixed (wave fic, item verify)
