### O512 — lang-parity decides the oracle-blind cap per side, so C#'s call_precision compares an incumbent scored with the exclusion against a dialect scored without it

- **Symptom:** `indexbench lang-parity -dialect csharp -incumbent csharpextract` reads call_precision .7925 for the incumbent (wave ld: .758) and .794 for the dialect: +.0015 where the dialect is far ahead
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** lang-parity on App-vNext/Polly 9ad5ae9b and JamesNK/Newtonsoft.Json 4e13299d (scip-dotnet 0.2.14, the name-dated Roslyn 5.9.0 dump), wave lh, 2026-10-02, with csharp opted in to LB-F175 (O464); per-side counts read from each side's reading
  - **Expected:** one rule for both sides of a comparison
  - **Actual:** the incumbent's blind pairs are 1,607 of 36,962 (4.3%: its `nameof(` sites, written as calls with no occurrence), under LB-F203's 10% cap, so they leave both counts (28,018 of 35,355 = .7925); the dialect's are 6,441 of 36,400 (17.7%), over the cap, so its rule is off (28,902 of 36,400 = .794; with the rule .9647, 28,902 of 29,959)
  - **Repro:** the parity run in `docs/bench/readings/lang/parity/README.md`
- **Root cause:** `cmdLangParity` builds each side's reading with `MergeSubject`, which applies LB-F203 to that side's own share
- **Fix:** none here (an owner decision on LB-F203's scope): decide the cap once per comparison (on the dialect's share, or the pooled share) and apply it to both sides
- **Status:** open (needs owner decision)
- **Impact:** none on C#'s verdict (call_precision is beyond its level_met L1, so not gated); a comparison whose sides fall either side of the cap at a gated level would be decided by the rule, not the extractors
