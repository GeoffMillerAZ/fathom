### O475 — "callers" is three different graphs: which surface answers from which, before and after wave lg

- **Symptom:** design §1.6 (wave le) graded Go's L2 on `callgraph.BuildBoth` as "what `callers` and `blast_radius` read"; the owner-architect decision (b) of 2026-10-02 grades "what callers/blast_radius return". Neither said which graph each surface reads, and they differ
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** reading the serving code at 90f0a01f: `internal/mcp/resolved_nav.go` (`loadNavGraphsErr`, `navGraphs.pick`, `blastRadiusResolved`), `internal/mcp/nav_tools.go` (symbol_context), `internal/mcp/tools.go` (find_callers), `internal/mcp/extra_tools.go` (blast_radius kinds), `internal/mcp/analysis_tools.go`, `internal/mcp/references_tool.go`, `internal/mcp/review_tool.go`, `internal/mcp/verify_plan_tool.go`, `cmd/fathom/verify.go#L455`, `internal/analysis/review/github.go#L127`, `internal/query/engine.go` (`FindCallers`)
  - **Expected:** one served graph behind every "who calls X" answer
  - **Actual:** three graphs and one store query, per surface:
    - `blast_radius` kind "change" (agent surface): by default the RESOLVED graph (`callgraph.BuildResolved`: the typed pass's direct, dispatch and func-value edges only) whenever the service has typed edges (`navGraphs.pick`); `include_heuristic: true`, or a service with no typed edges, reads GraphBoth (`BuildBoth`). by_depth walks the same graph. Kind "rename" lists callers from GraphSyntax (`callgraph.Build` alone, `RenameImpact`) and tallies tiers on GraphBoth; kind "delete" unions the store's call index with the resolved callers
    - `find_callers` (extended surface): by default the resolved graph's direct callers (`ResolvedCallersAcross`, BP-26); with `include_heuristic: true` the rows come from the store's call index (`query.Engine.FindCallers`: observations whose raw `Calls` strings name the symbol, no graph at all) and only the depth/confidence annotation from GraphBoth
    - `symbol_context` (agent surface): callers, callees and related_tests from GraphSyntax (`callgraph.Build`, option "A resolved": .7480 / .7327 on cobra and cli/cli), with the resolution tally and `typed_edges` from GraphBoth
    - GraphBoth (`BuildBoth`) also answers `references` (mention confidence), `review_diff` (resolution, layers, receipt), `test_impact`, `localize`, the console, the findings tools, `verify_plan` and `fathom verify` (test_reaches, edge_wired, product reach), the review engine's changed-symbol callers, test gap, certify portfolio, centrality and conserve
  - **Repro:** follow each handler to its `memo.GraphFor` kind or `callgraph.Build*` call
- **Root cause:** BP-26/BP-27 (resolved by default) changed the agent tools' default graph one handler at a time, and symbol_context's lists were never moved off the syntax graph; the L2 decision named "the served graph" without the map
- **Fix:** wave lg records the map (ADR-048 amendment 2026-10-02, owner-architect decision (b); design §1.6) and makes GraphBoth the one served graph for every list above it names: E and D now hold inside `BuildBoth` itself (LB-F199, LB-F200), and symbol_context's callers, callees and related_tests read GraphBoth (`ng.Both`, which is the syntax graph when a service has no typed edges or they failed to load). For Go, the DEFAULT agent answer of `blast_radius` and `find_callers` stays the typed graph (option B: .9805 / .9180); GraphBoth (option C, graded: .9573 / .9219) is what `symbol_context`, `blast_radius` with `include_heuristic`, verify and review read. `find_callers` with `include_heuristic` still lists the store's raw `Calls` matches (outside this wave: the store side is under the table freeze)
- **Status:** closed 2026-10-02 (map recorded; symbol_context moved to the served graph)
- **Impact:** Go's L2 now grades a set that agent surfaces actually return; the remaining divergence (find_callers' heuristic rows) is named, not hidden
