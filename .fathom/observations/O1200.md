### O1200 — The outline join misses its 150 ms deadline on a cold tree-sitter grammar

- **Symptom:** `readout.Outline` over one file: Go (go/parser) under 1 ms, Markdown 0.3 ms, but Bash took 161 ms on its first use in a process and 3.6 ms after; `fusion.New` itself 11 ms.
- **Evidence:** a scratch timing test in the ro1c clone (2026-10-18): fusion.New(nil, nil, Config{}) then ExtractForBench twice per file.
- **Root cause:** the tags extractors compile their WASM grammar on first use per process, and `fathom readout-hook` is a one-shot process per Bash call.
- **Fix:** none built (no cache under the table freeze). RO-M1 should read the hook rows' `joins_missed` containing `outline` by language before read-outline is judged; a lazily kept compiled grammar is the lever if the miss rate is high.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** in the hook, read-outline on a tree-sitter language will often serve `outline: not computed (deadline)`; replay computes with no deadline, so its figures can read better than the hook's.
