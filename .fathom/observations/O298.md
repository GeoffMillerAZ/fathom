### O298 — layer_coverage names no reason a section is uncompiled

- **Symptom:** 8 sections stayed uncompiled across passes with nothing saying why; a failed section was not even in `uncompiled_scopes`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** obligations `layer_coverage`, compile_layer
  - **Expected:** each uncompiled section carries its reason (failed, prefiltered, refused, budget, grew after the run).
  - **Actual:** only the document paths of the budget tail and the prefiltered sections, no reason.
  - **Repro:** TestLayerCoverageNamesWhyEachSectionIsUncompiled.
- **Root cause:** the run recorded no per-section reason and the failed list was never folded into the uncompiled tail.
- **Fix:** EW-35: `compile_runs.uncompiled_reasons_json` (an added column), `LayerCoverage.UncompiledReasons`, served as `uncompiled_reasons`.
- **Status:** fixed — EW-35
- **Impact:** an operator could not tell a failed section from a budget stop from corpus growth.
