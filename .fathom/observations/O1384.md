### O1384 — AP-3 and AP-4 cannot verify by symbol: their symbols live under third_party/, which .fathomignore keeps out of the index

- **Symptom:** `fathom verify` reports AP-3 and AP-4 unproven although foldFragments and checkFragments exist and their tests pass.
- **Evidence:** third_party/agentic-kit/cmd/release-notes/fragments.go defines both; .fathomignore excludes third_party/ (the ADR-070 mirror).
- **Root cause:** a symbol tag needs an indexed symbol; the mirror is deliberately not indexed.
- **Fix:** owner-architect decision 2026-10-20: switch only the two rows' tags to lexical oracles on the mirror file (`^func foldFragments\(`, `^func checkFragments\(`), keeping ids, text and needs. Behaviour stays pinned by the kit's own tests (TestFoldFragmentsSortedBreakingFirst, TestCheckFragmentsRefusesBadHeadingsAndStrayUnreleasedLines) and fathom's `task changelog-check`.
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** the lexical oracle proves the function exists, not what it does.
