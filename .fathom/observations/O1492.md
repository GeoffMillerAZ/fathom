### O1492 — The hook log cannot say how many served rows were raw: only the call log's citations can, and only as a bound

- **Symptom:** RS-M4 asks for the raw share of served rows over 14 days of the hook log, but `~/.fathom/hooks.jsonl`'s answered beats record only `claims` (claim-bearing rows), not total rows or tier.
- **Evidence:** `docs/bench/readings/rule-selection/raw-share.md`: the call log's `pointers` give each served row's citation; matching them to stored claim cites reads 50.6% unresolved, but serving prepends at most 2 raw rows per call, so the raw share is at most 38.9%.
- **Root cause:** neither log names a served row's tier; a row whose claim was recompiled away looks raw.
- **Fix:** none here (outside RS rows). A tier field on the call log's rows would make the reading exact.
- **Status:** open.
- **Impact:** ADR-077's third revisit trigger (raw above 50%) can only be read as a bound.
