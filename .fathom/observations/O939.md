### O939 — The pooled Wilson rule cannot help an ecosystem with one small subject

- **Symptom:** RF-M8's TypeScript stratum held one subject, zustand (13 of 13 collected tests read `test`, precision 1.000), and failed the Wilson bar at 0.772: a stratum of one is that subject, and 13 files cannot reach a 0.90 lower bound (n >= 35 at perfect precision).
- **Evidence:** docs/bench/readings/repository-facts/roles-r1.md (pooled strata table); O881's rule as pre-registered pools only an ecosystem's subjects under 35.
- **Root cause:** subjects were chosen by popularity and runner before any read, not by size, so an ecosystem's small subjects can number one.
- **Fix:** none in rf2 (the verdict does not turn on it: react-hook-form and Python's stratum fail on precision). A later reading either picks subjects with at least 35 collected tests each by a stated pre-read count, or pools across ecosystems for the interval bar and says why.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** any later per-ecosystem reading with a Wilson bar.
