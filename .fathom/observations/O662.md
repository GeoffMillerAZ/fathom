### O662 — finished plans sit unarchived and cost every pre-push verify run (custodian blind spot: finished-plan-unarchived)

- **Symptom:** 49 top-level `docs/plans/*.md` had zero open boxes on 2026-10-09; the pre-push gate runs `fathom verify` over every one of them, so each finished plan keeps costing verify time until it is archived
- **Evidence:** wave w2a: `fathom verify --strict` on a scratch ingest of HEAD passed 46 of 47 plans (retire-unused-analyzers failed: its `gone` rows need a clean tree at ingest); `fathom plans archive` then archived 22 and refused 23 on id absorption. Unabsorbed row ids per refused plan, sorted: 1 1 1 1 2 3 3 3 4 4 5 5 6 6 6 7 10 13 14 15 21 22 27 (median 6; four plans, the expert-suite-j/p/q/r series, have none of their ids in CHANGELOG.md, ADR.md, docs/changelog or a commit body). A crude lexical read predicted five fully absorbed; four archived and h1-coding-agent-excellence (15 ids unabsorbed) did not
- **Root cause:** nothing flags a plan that is finished but unarchived, and the absorption check at archive time is lexical on ids, so plans whose ids never reached the changelog stay held for want of prose, not of proof
- **Fix:** 22 plans archived in the K13 wave; the custodian needs a `finished-plan-unarchived` concern (a plan with zero open boxes and a passing `verify --strict`, older than a week), which this observation names and does not build; the 23 held plans need their ids absorbed by the release that shipped them, never by lines written only to satisfy the check
- **Status:** open (28 archived: 22 in w2a, 6 more in h1 after their ids were found on the released changelog lines that describe them; 21 finished plans still held for id absorption, retire-unused-analyzers among them, which now verifies 18 of 18 on a clean ingest; cli-kit-adoption finished in h1 and is not yet offered)
- **Impact:** pre-push verify time grows with every finished plan left in `docs/plans/`
