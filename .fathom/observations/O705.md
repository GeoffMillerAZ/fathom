### O705 — lang-oracle's merge keeps a re-measured subject's superseded notes beside the new ones

- **Symptom:** re-measuring a subject into an existing reading (`-out` merge) left the old per-subject note next to its replacement: github-actions held both "pytorch/pytorch: 2289 tool-oracle entries ..." and "... 2338 ...", serverless two "restricted to" lists (113 and 114 files)
- **Evidence:** wave c2's re-measure of docs/bench/readings/lang/github-actions.json and serverless.json; a fresh `-out` reproduced every automatic note of the github-actions reading with only the current count
- **Root cause:** the merge unions notes by exact text, and a subject's automatic notes embed counts and file lists, so a changed value is a new note rather than a replacement of the subject's note
- **Fix:** none here (cmd/indexbench is outside this item); the wave c2 readings were written fresh (github-actions) or had the one superseded note dropped by hand (serverless). A merge that replaces the notes a re-measured subject generates (they begin with its name) would end it
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a re-measured reading can carry two contradicting counts for one subject unless the measurer notices
