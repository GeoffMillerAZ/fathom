### O1247 — "I42's bytes under any kill" cannot hold byte for byte where a live kill refuses an ask or a site is closed

- **Symptom:** JG-5 (docs/plans/judgment-foundations.md) and ADR-073 decision 5 say a killed port serves "I42's bytes" on every surface. Byte identity with provider `none` holds only where the port is `none` at Open (`FATHOM_DECIDE=off`, a refused consent) or the site is never asked (the obligations envelope under `deny_sites` or `sites`). A live kill (the kill file, the user's `enabled = false`) refuses an ask the port received, and a closed `route` site answers before asking. Both serve the none-shaped payload plus their own disclosure: `POST /route` keeps its `error` naming the closure (pinned by `TestRouteHandler`'s "discloses why" case), the obligations envelope keeps `rank_note` and `ranked_count`, and `certify suggest` prints its summary line instead of the provider-required line.
- **Evidence:** `TestNoJudgedTokenOnAnySurfaceUnderKill` (cmd/fathom/decidedby_surfaces_test.go, wave dmp1) checks every kill source for zero `judged` tokens, no `decided_by` and no dial. It checks full bytes against the I42 golden only where the port was not asked. Elsewhere it checks that the decision-bearing fields match provider none's (route's shape, verb, confidence, band and provider, the obligations `ranked_by` and rules).
- **Root cause:** the contract's wording, not the code. The kill's `killed` line and the per-answer error are separate disclosures that KS-2 and ADR-044 decision 8 kept on purpose.
- **Fix:** none here. If byte identity under a live kill is wanted, drop route's `error` and the obligations `rank_note` when every failure is a `*decide.KilledError`. That needs a decision, because it removes a pinned disclosure.
- **Status:** resolved (wave dmp2, rails2; owner-architect decision): the kill's per-answer disclosure stays (route's `error`, the obligations `rank_note`), and the contract's wording moves instead. JG-5's row text and ADR-073's amendment of 2026-10-18 say a live kill serves I42's decision fields byte-identical, and full I42 bytes where the port is `none` at Open or the site is never asked.
- **Impact:** a reader of "I42's bytes" could expect a killed daemon's `/route` body to equal the unconfigured one. It equals it in every decision field, and only the `error` text differs.
