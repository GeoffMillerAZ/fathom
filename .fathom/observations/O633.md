### O633 — a registered basename needs a connector `extClassMap` entry (TestLanguageRegistryParity): two connector lines are an item-ci edit outside its file list

- **Symptom:** registering `action.yml`, `action.yaml` and `composer.json` in `fusion.New` failed TestLanguageRegistryParity until `internal/connector/connector.go` classified them
- **Evidence:** "extractor(s) registered in fusion.New with no language classification in connector.extClassMap"
- **Root cause:** one registry, two files (ADR-021 §1): a basename dispatch is declared in fusion and classified in the connector
- **Fix:** four map lines (`action.yml`, `action.yaml` with the existing "yaml" tag, `composer.json` with "json"), no new language tag, so connector's languages_test and typededges.go are untouched
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** a file named `action.yml` or `composer.json` keeps the yaml or json language tag in listings; its source kinds (GHAActionDef, ComposerPackage) say what it is
