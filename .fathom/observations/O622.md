### O622 — a rule file claimed by content alone never reaches the route pass, so its label matchers join nothing at ingest

- **Symptom:** thanos' `examples/alerts/alerts.yaml` (claimed by the content sniff, no manifest name) stores its rules, but none of its `job=` matchers can become a route edge, even toward a declared Service
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fusion.isRouteCandidate` (routeref.go), `TestIngest_PrometheusRouteRefs` (which checks name-claimed files only)
  - **Actual:** the route pass reads paths a manifest claims by extension, basename or glob; a content claim is not a path claim
  - **Repro:** `monitoring/more.yaml` in `prometheusRepo()`: read as Prometheus, its `job="checkout"` target never resolved
- **Root cause:** route candidacy predates content-claimed route producers
- **Fix:** item mesh's routeref change (route candidacy by content) lifts it with no change to the dialect
- **Status:** open (item mesh)
- **Impact:** Prometheus rule files named `*.rules.yml`, `*_rules.yml` or `prometheus.yml` join today; others after mesh's change
