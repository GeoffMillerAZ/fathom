### O549 — the tier-C Ruby calls oracle's own locals.scm defines no rescue variable and scopes no `def self.x`: 26 of its pairs are its own errors

- **Symptom:** after LB-F254 the dialect and the oracle (upstream tags.scm with locals.scm, tier C) still differ by 96 pairs on sinatra + jekyll. Of these, 26 come from the oracle's locals query
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect ruby -tool-oracle <ts-tags calls> -unmatched` (wave lx, fathom cfa58b63)
  - **Expected:** `e` in `rescue => e; e.message` is a local, and a local of `def self.body` is not seen in `def self.call`
  - **Actual:** 24 oracle-only pairs call a rescue variable (`e`, `error`), and 2 fathom-only pairs (`engines`, `body`) are calls the oracle reads as locals leaked from a singleton method
  - **Repro:** calibration/oracles/ts-tags/README.md (Ruby), then the -unmatched output
- **Root cause:** tree-sitter-ruby v0.23.1's queries/locals.scm has no `exception_variable` definition, and its only gate scope is `method` (no `singleton_method`, class or module)
- **Fix:** none (the oracle is upstream's, run as published). The dialect's locals.scm follows Ruby, and the reading classifies the difference
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a ceiling of about .997 on the tier-C call_recall the dialect can match while staying correct
