### O665 — intent living docs ran 2.6 to 5.8 times past the kit's ~150-line budget, and the parser was keyed on base names

- **Symptom:** docs/intent/futures.md was 873 lines (66 records), scope.md 411 (30 goals), domain.md 221; the project-intent kit budgets a living doc at about 150
- **Evidence:** K8 reading of 2026-10-09; `internal/intent/parse.go` parseLivingDoc dispatched goals, the futures shape check and invariants by `filepath.Base(rel)`, console search read futures from docs/intent/futures.md only, and the progress page keyed its goal cache on scope.md alone, so a split would have parsed members as nothing and a router as an empty futures.md
- **Root cause:** no instrument measured the budget, and every reader assumed one file per living doc
- **Fix:** the corpus reads docs/intent/<doc>.md plus docs/intent/<doc>/*.md as one document (record and condition numbering carried across members); `fathom intent audit` U15 names an id defined but unrouted, routed but undefined, or defined twice; console search, the progress cache key and the intent tab follow the family; futures, scope and domain split behind routers of at most 150 lines with every record byte-intact and the audit's counts unchanged; a max_lines concern per intent doc and member (w2a2 routers, AG-33, AG-35 to AG-37)
- **Status:** fixed by the w2a2 routers branch
- **Impact:** every intent-design session loaded up to 873 lines to read one future
