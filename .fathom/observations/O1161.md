### O1161 — one plan's daemon verify missing its deadline fails `fathom plans status` whole, after 2m21s, with no table

- **Symptom:** on the deployed daemon v0.15.3 (78157704), `fathom plans status --service fathom` failed after 2m21s with `error: unwrap verify_plan response: RPC error: abandoned: the call's deadline passed (the index-class server deadline or X-Fathom-Deadline)` and printed no table: one plan's verify error discarded every other plan's report.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom plans status --service fathom` (daemon mode, the bounded fan-out of O29 in cmd/fathom/plans.go).
  - **Expected:** a plan the daemon could not verify reads as unmeasured on its own row, every other plan reports, and the verb exits 2 (operational, docs/plans/README.md) rather than 1 with nothing.
  - **Actual:** exit 1, no rows. Single-plan `fathom verify` through the same daemon, 2026-10-17: language-breadth 366 s, responsible-tenant 54 s, surface-reckoning 16 s, observatory-console 11 s, retire-unused-analyzers 3.9 s. Swap was 15.9 of 16 GiB and app-kit ingests were running at the time.
  - **Repro:** `time fathom plans status --service fathom` while the daemon is loaded; `time fathom verify --service fathom --plan docs/plans/language-breadth.md`.
- **Root cause:** two causes.
  1. `computePlanReports` returned the first per-plan verify error as the whole run's error (`for _, e := range verifyErrs { if e != nil { return nil, e } }`), and `plans next` shares that path.
  2. The 366 s is the plan's size multiplied by the daemon path. language-breadth has 768 rows, and the 32 KB dispatch budget keeps about 13 per verify_plan answer, so one verify takes 54 calls. Each call rebuilds the call graph under shed (O1014, which has the profile). Each follow-up also carried every row still missing, so the daemon re-verified up to 755 rows to answer 13. That is about 20,000 row verifications for one 768-row plan, and 24% of the profile. Store mode on the same fresh store: 8.6 to 10.7 s, v0.15.2 and v0.15.3 alike, so this is no v0.15.3 regression.
- **Fix:** wave fx5, plansdl, 2026-10-17. Two changes:
  1. A plan whose daemon verify errors reads `not measured: <the error>` on its own row and in `--json` `not_measured`. Every other plan reports, one summary line counts the unmeasured plans, and the verb exits 2. `plans next` names the plan on stderr and exits 2. `plans archive` still errors.
  2. A follow-up call carries no more missing rows than the last cut answer held, so the rows the daemon verifies stay linear in the plan's size.
  The graph rebuild per call (O1014) and the daemon's false failures at refuse (O1162) remain.
- **Status:** fixed (the verb). The daemon-path cost is tracked in O1014.
- **Impact:** the computed progress source AGENTS.md names goes dark whenever the largest plan outlasts the daemon's deadline, which is exactly when the machine is loaded.
