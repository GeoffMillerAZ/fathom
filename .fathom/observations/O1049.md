### O1049 — A Service selector with no workload in the corpus now counts as an undeclared route reference

- **Symptom:** after the route side pass stores a core Service's `spec.selector` (the ServiceMonitor hop of observed-by, TL-15), `RouteRefUnresolved[undeclared]` rises by one per Service whose selected pods the corpus does not hold (a Helm-templated or operator-created workload, a Service for an external backend).
- **Evidence:** `TestIngest_RouteRefs` 1 to 4 and `TestMongobetweenLaunchRoutes` 2 to 3 undeclared (their fixtures carry Services with selectors and no pod template); `TestIngest_PrometheusRuleJoinWithheld` had to drop its Services' selectors to keep asserting that rules add no count.
- **Root cause:** `Service/<ns>/<name> service_selector` is a route-selector reference like the AuthorizationPolicy's; an unmatched selector is undeclared by the pass's own rule.
- **Fix:** none needed for observed-by; if the undeclared total reads as noise on a manifest-heavy subject, report Service selectors under their own reason rather than dropping the edge.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a route-ref census on Kubernetes subjects reads a higher undeclared count after the epoch bump that stores the edge; stored edges and every other count are unchanged.
