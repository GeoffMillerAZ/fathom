### O1439 — a /** */ comment inside a Prisma block is an error to the pinned grammar

- **Symptom:** the papermark holdout (`docs/bench/readings/lang/holdout/prisma-papermark.json`) reads sym_recall .9971 (1,384 of 1,388) and ref_recall .9957 (464 of 466): model `ReferralInvite` in `prisma/schema/referral.prisma` loses `scopeId`, `email`, `referralLink` and `sentAt`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -unmatched` on mfts/papermark ed19717e: the four fields are oracle_only, on lines 19 to 22, right after a `/** ... */` comment at lines 12 to 18 inside the model
- **Root cause:** victorhqc/tree-sitter-prisma at 3556b2c1 reads `//` and `///` comments only; a block comment inside a block is a syntax error, and error recovery drops the members after it. The Prisma schema engine accepts it.
- **Fix:** none here (a holdout is never tuned against). `LB-Q-prisma-rest` names it: a grammar pin that parses the comment, or a pre-parse step that blanks it at equal byte length.
- **Status:** open
