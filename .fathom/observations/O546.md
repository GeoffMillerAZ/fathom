### O546 — on Sorbet-typed gems the resolver draws 63 of 1,027 and 54 of 680 cross-file edges: a constant binds only through its own file's requires, and these gems require from entry files

- **Symptom:** file_edge_recall .0613 (ruby-lsp v0.26.11) and .0794 (spoom v1.8.10) against scip-ruby's cross-file references, precision 1 on both
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect ruby -scip <index>` (wave lx, fathom cfa58b63)
  - **Expected:** a reference to a constant defined in another file of the gem is a file edge, as Sorbet resolves it
  - **Actual:** file_edges_fathom 63 and 54, file_edges_truth 1,027 and 680
  - **Repro:** calibration/oracles/scip-ruby/README.md, section "Sorbet-typed subjects"
- **Root cause:** fileresolve.go's callEdges looks a constant up lexically among the names the file's own requires bring (imports.constants "lexical", LB-F76). `ruby_lsp/internal.rb` (77 requires) and `spoom.rb` with its subsystem entries require every file once, so most files require nothing and their constants bind nothing
- **Fix:** none here (internal/fusion is never edited by a language item). A constant could bind through the files that require its file (the load-order closure), or to the one service file that defines it at the top of its lexical path
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a re-pin of Ruby's graded subjects to typed gems loses L3, so the reading keeps sinatra and jekyll (F.29)
