### O1485 — On the fleet daemon the first call of a tool after an idle spell pays seconds of page-ins: most of its memory sits compressed or in swap

- **Symptom:** the same call is slow first and fast right after on the deployed daemon, on tools that read no cold index: `find_symbol` 6.01 s then 0.03 s, `read_source` 1.52 s then 0.02 s. A `search` with no hits took 2.08 and 2.21 s there against 0.22 s on a scratch serve-http over the same tree.
- **Evidence:** read-only, 2026-10-20 14:03 to 14:12Z, deployed 21fc4b309 at tenancy level normal. `/statusz`: footprint 1.37 GB; `ps`: 430 MB resident, so about 1 GB of the daemon was compressed or swapped. Host: 105 MB free, 7.0 GB in the compressor, 5.1 of 6 GB swap used, load 6 to 9, with other agents' test binaries holding 1.2 to 1.7 GB each. The 10.06 s cold `search` that O1447 measured at 13:33Z ran while the daemon refreshed fathom (typed-edge pass heap 3,135 MiB at 13:34:18Z) and went to shed at 13:34:21Z.
- **Root cause:** the host's memory level, not a fathom code path: the OS compresses or swaps the idle daemon's pages and any call that touches them pages them back in. The search-path half of O1447's deployed gap (ripgrep's single-threaded `--sort path` run on a cold page cache) is fixed there; this is what remains.
- **Fix:** none here: it lies outside the tool paths and inside the table and cache freeze. The tenancy ladder already reads `phys_footprint`, which counts compressed pages; whether the daemon should hold less resident between calls is RT territory once RT-M1 reads.
- **Status:** open.
- **Impact:** any first call after a pause can cost seconds on a loaded host, whatever the tool, so a slow figure measured there says little about the tool's own path until it is repeated.
