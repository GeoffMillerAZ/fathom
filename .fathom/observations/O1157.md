- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `internal/scenariobench` (`checkRuleSourcePin`), O1153's "Not covered" line
  - **Expected:** every `path#Lnn` rule source names where its text moved and the repin command.
  - **Actual:** 8 pins in `testdata/scenariobench/guards-fathom.json` and 4 in `guards-m1.json` had no `rule.needle` (needle was required only for ADR.md).

- **Fix:** each of the 12 pins carries a `rule.needle` (the cited line's own opening text, first occurrence verified to be the pinned line); `checkRuleSourcePin` now requires a needle on every `path#Lnn` source (`TestCheckRuleSourcePin_LinePinNeedsNeedle`).
