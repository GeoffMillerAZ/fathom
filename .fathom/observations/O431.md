### O431 — the kyverno dialect claims chainsaw assertion fragments and defines a policy in each

- **Symptom:** against Kyverno's own loader (wave ox, `calibration/oracles/kyverno`, kyverno v1.18.1 `policy.Load`), the kyverno reading's sym_precision over every claimed file is .7262: 771 of fathom's 2,827 definitions on the two kyverno/policies pins are no Kyverno entity
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-policies -dialect kyverno` on kyverno/policies main ec478cb5 (331 of 508 definitions) and release-1.18 4422f711 (1,722 of 2,319), no `-only`
  - **Expected:** a file whose documents are chainsaw assertions about a policy (`.chainsaw-test/policy-ready.yaml`, `chainsaw-step-NN-assert-N.yaml`: the policy's `apiVersion`, `kind`, `metadata.name` and a `status` to wait for) declares no policy; Kyverno's loader refuses 341 of them (JMESPath keys such as `(conditions[?type == 'Ready'])` fail the CRD schema) and skips 431 documents (no `spec`)
  - **Actual:** `fusion.PolicyDialect` claims every such file (every document is in a Kyverno API group) and the dialect emits a `policy` definition per document, a second declaration of a policy its sibling file declares
  - **Repro:** the kyverno reading's command in `calibration/oracles/kyverno/README.md` without `-only`; the reading's notes carry both measurements
- **Root cause:** the claim and the dialect read a policy from `apiVersion`, `kind` and `metadata.name` alone; chainsaw's partial objects (assert and error steps) carry exactly those
- **Fix:** open, for the next wave: a Kyverno policy entity needs a `spec` (Kyverno's loader skips a policy without one), or the claim leaves `.chainsaw-test*` assertion files (a chainsaw `Test`'s assert/error steps name them) to the kubernetes dialect
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** the reading of record measures the files outside `.chainsaw-test*` (L1 and L2 excellent); `find_symbol` on a policy name answers with up to two spurious declarations per policy that has a chainsaw test
