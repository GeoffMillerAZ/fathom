### O524 — PHP L2 graded at tier B from the php-parser dump reads call_precision 1 and level L4; the scip-php (tier A) reading stays .6924

- **Symptom:** with `-calls-from tool` (LB-F204) php.json computes level_met L4 (L1 excellent, L2 good, L3 excellent, L4 excellent), where the scip-php grading read L1 with L2 unmet.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect php -scip <index.scip> -tool-oracle <php-parser dump with calls> -calls-from tool` on guzzle/guzzle 7.9.3 and Seldaek/monolog 3.9.0 with scip-php 71a5b117, php-parser 5.7.0, PHP 8.5.11, composer 2.10.3; the same run without `-calls-from tool` (definitions-only dump) reproduces call_precision .6924, 1,869 fathom-only pairs and 0 oracle-only.
  - **Environment:** composer dev dependencies installed per calibration/oracles/scip-php/README.md (guzzle minus client-integration-tests, monolog after `config lock true`); index sizes 827 KB and 2.4 MB as before; no gap explains a pair.
- **Root cause:** none to fix: scip-php writes no occurrence for a method called on a receiver it cannot type (O467). The tier-B grade is a syntactic agreement of the dialect and the dump over the same grammar's call nodes (the Kotlin PSI precedent), not a resolved-call proof; the oracle-blind share is ~31% of fathom's pairs, over LB-F175's 10% cap.
- **Fix:** none; the reading carries both numbers and LB-Q-php flips on the computed level.
- **Status:** open for the integrator to confirm before push that a syntactic tier-B L2 grade is wanted for PHP
- **Impact:** PHP's level is L4 on D2's tier-B ceiling (L2 good); tier-A L2 stays unmet until scip-php resolves vendor-typed receivers.
