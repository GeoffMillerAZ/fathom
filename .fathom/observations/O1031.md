### O1031 — The kubernetes doc dialect and kubectl oracle read no CronJob job-template image, the incumbent now does

- **Symptom:** after LB-F451 the stored extraction (K8sExtractor) names a CronJob's container images as K8sContainer nodes, but the kubernetes doc dialect kubernetes.json measures reads containers only at `spec` and `spec.template.spec`, and the kubectl oracle's `containerLists` mirror those two paths.
- **Evidence:** internal/extractor/dialects/kubernetes/doc.json's four `data_source` entities; calibration/oracles/kubernetes/kubectl.go `containerLists`; TestK8sContainerFactsAllThreePodPathsInitContainersDistinct (acme/wait:1, acme/report:3 at the CronJob's line).
- **Root cause:** the dialect and its oracle were written to the image collector's two paths (k8sextract.go:149-152 before LB-F451); the job template was never a data-source path.
- **Fix:** add `$.spec.jobTemplate.spec.template.spec.containers[*]` and `initContainers[*]` to doc.json and the same two lists to `containerLists` in one change, then re-read kubernetes.json. None of kubernetes.json's six subjects holds a CronJob at its pin, so no reading moves; it waits for a subject that does.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** none measured today; on a repository with CronJobs the measured dialect reads fewer images than ingest stores.
