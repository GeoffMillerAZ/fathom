### O1303 — the pre-edit blast radius never answers unless another caller built the graph, which caps the hook-path answered share near 50%

- **Symptom:** in RT-M14's harness run on the managed daemon (4823561d), all 50 pre-edit `blast_radius` calls were declined in 0 ms. Every `verify_plan` answered except the first stop invocation's two partials, so 49 of 101 hook graph calls answered (48.5%), under the row's 50% bar.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `blast_radius` from the pre-edit hook (`cmd/fathom/edithook_blast.go`, 500 ms cap); `/metrics` `fathom_cache_events_total{group="graphstore"}`
  - **Expected:** some hook-path blast-radius calls answer from a resident graph
  - **Actual:** 0 of 50 answered. At the run's start, graphstore's only eviction since the daemon started was a refuse drop (`evicted_refuse` 1), with budget, shed, idle and watermark evictions at 0. The internal disk read 4.7 GiB free at 15:36Z, under the 5 GiB floor. Nothing rebuilt the graph before or during the run.
  - **Repro:** `docs/bench/readings/efficiency-wave/hook-path-harness.py --url http://127.0.0.1:7575 ... --probes 0` while the service's call graph is not resident; reading `docs/bench/readings/tenancy/rt-m14-2026-10-19.md`
- **Root cause:** RT-191 declines a flight the caller cannot use. 500 ms is under half of graphstore's 2 s expected cost and under any build of this graph (seconds), so the hook can never start the build. It answers only from a graph that another caller built at the current watermark and that survived evictions. Of the two graph-backed hook calls, then, only `verify_plan` can answer, and the share sits at about 50%. RT-M14's K2 reads `evictions{reason}` for budget/shed or watermark. A refuse-dominant case, the swap-volume floor dropping the graph, is not named.
- **Fix:** not decided. Options in the plan's own terms: keep the graph resident after a refuse drop clears (K2's lazy syntax graph and `Options.Priority`), or drop the pre-edit trailer from the hook path where it cannot answer (EW-76's "the call dropped where the hook gains nothing"). Either way, K2 should name the refuse case.
- **Status:** open
