### O1165 -- the four ADR-010 group surfaces drew zero calls in 103 days; retire them as a `### Breaking` change

- **Symptom:** ADR-018's second Revisit condition ("kept for taxonomy completeness pending the 30-day post-flip log") has fired. The `coding`, `investigation`, `shared` and `platform` surfaces are un-killed, documented, and unused.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** the daemon's configured call log (`~/.fathom/daemon/*/record.json` `call_log`, resolved by `internal/console.ResolveCallLog`, rule `daemon`), read in place, counts only, nothing committed.
  - **Expected:** some traffic on at least one group surface within a 30-day clock.
  - **Actual:** 124,487 records from 2026-07-07T05:23Z to 2026-10-18T03:38Z (103 days). Of the 19,246 records that carry a `surface` field, 0 name a group surface (agent 18,748, cli 456, extended 29, companion 12, modules 1). The 105,241 records without a `surface` predate the field and carry only the tool's own `group`, so their surface cannot be attributed.
  - **Repro:** group the log by `surface`; count values in {coding, investigation, shared, platform}.
- **Root cause:** the default eight-tool `agent` surface (ADR-018 amendment) absorbed every real caller; the group surfaces were kept only for taxonomy.
- **Fix:** proposed, not made (a records wave retires nothing): remove the four group surfaces and their registrations, update `docs/mcp-tools.md`, and ship it as a `### Breaking` CHANGELOG line with a migration note naming `/rpc/agent` and `/rpc/extended`. Check the removal against the compatibility surface in `docs/intent/scope.md` first, since it deletes shipped endpoints. Revisit the strictSurfaces multi-tenant option (ADR-018 Revisit 2, F8) in the same wave so it is not orphaned.
- **Status:** open (proposed for a later wave, 2026-10-17).
- **Impact:** four surfaces and their tests and docs are maintained for zero measured use.
