### O698 — the seam's Mount Targets name files, but Symfony imports a directory recursively and DRF registers a class of another module

- **Symptom:** symfony/demo reads 12 of 19 routes at their served path; NetBox misses 50 DRF `@action` routes and reads 106 extra routes of read-only viewsets, and misses 792 bulk routes of its router subclass
- **Evidence:** `config/routes.yaml` imports `routing.controllers` (the project's `src/Controller/`) under `/{_locale}`; Symfony loads that directory recursively, `Compose`'s directory Target names only its direct files, so `src/Controller/Admin/BlogController.php` keeps its local paths. A scratch build whose directory Target includes subdirectories reads 19 of 19, bound recall and precision 1.0 (`docs/bench/readings/routes/php.md`). On NetBox, `router.register('devices', views.DeviceViewSet)` names a class the `urls.py` cannot see: its `@action` methods and its base classes (a `ReadOnlyModelViewSet` has no create, update or destroy) are in `views.py`, which holds many viewsets, so a mount of the module would serve every viewset's routes under every prefix; `NetBoxRouter(DefaultRouter)` in `netbox/api/routers.py` remaps the list route to bulk actions for every `urls.py` that instantiates it (`docs/bench/readings/routes/python.md`)
- **Root cause:** the Target forms (`./x`, `../x`, `/x`, a dotted module, a directory's direct files) name files; neither a recursive directory nor a class within a module is expressible, and a reader sees one file
- **Fix:** proposed, not built (seam, `compose.go`): a recursive directory Target (the amendment O693 proposes for Java's deployment roots; one condition in `resolvePath`), and a class-scoped Target (`./views#DeviceViewSet`) whose file's routes a reader files under the class, so a viewset's actions and `@action` extras compose under its register prefix. The router subclass needs a reader that reports a router class's mapping, a contract form of its own
- **Status:** open for Django only: the Symfony half closed in wave lf2 (a recursive `/**` directory Target, LB-F351; symfony/demo 19 of 19, bound recall and precision 1.0, LB-F355). NetBox re-read unchanged (.4284 / .9424): the class-scoped Target and the router-subclass form are not built
- **Impact:** a Symfony app with controllers in subdirectories, and a DRF app whose viewsets live outside its `urls.py`, read routes at a wrong path, or miss or invent methods, until the seam grows these forms
