### O716 — k8sextract reads no List kind: a ConfigMapList's ConfigMaps are no symbols (2026-10-10)

- **Symptom:** kube-prometheus' `grafana-dashboardDefinitions.yaml` (`kind: ConfigMapList`, 33 ConfigMaps under `items`) stores no `ConfigMap/monitoring/<name>` symbol; only the documents the items embed are read (wave c3's k8sembed.go)
- **Evidence:** TestIngest_K8sEmbeddedDocuments asserts the Secret and GrafanaDashboard objects beside their embedded documents, and could not assert the ConfigMapList's items; `internal/extractor/k8sextract.go` has no `items` or `List` handling
- **Root cause:** k8sextract reads a document's top-level object only; a `*List` kind is kubectl's wrapper for several objects in one document
- **Fix:** none here (k8sextract.go is the incumbent the kubernetes dialect will replace); the kubernetes dialect's dispatch should select `$.items[*]` of a List kind
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a ConfigMap shipped in a List is invisible to find_symbol and the policy pass's label joins
