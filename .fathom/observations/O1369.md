### O1369 — the extractor epoch's data digest costs 0.49 ms once per process

- **Symptom:** ADR-075 D4 derives `ExtractorEpoch` from the embedded dialect data, computed at `internal/fusion` package init, so every process that links fusion (the CLI, serve-http, the hooks) pays the digest once.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `go test -run XXX -bench BenchmarkEmbeddedDataDigestCold -benchtime 200x ./internal/extractor/` (Apple M4)
  - **Reading:** 488970 ns/op: 238 files, 807692 bytes (143 `dialects/*/*.json`, 94 `dialects/*/*.scm`, `grammars.lock.json`); no grammar blob is read, the lock pins their sha256
- **Root cause:** sha256 over the embedded bytes; the reads are in-memory copies of the embed.
- **Fix:** none needed: `EmbeddedDataDigest` runs once (`sync.OnceValue`), `epochOf` once at init.
- **Status:** closed (measured)
