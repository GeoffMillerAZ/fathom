### O707 — twelve handlers do work before Parse, so their help is a static list

- **Symptom:** help cannot hand `-h` to the kill-switch hooks (edit, prompt, stop, session, session-end, pre-compact), serve-http, `certify suggest`, `plans context`, `plans lease`, `plans outcome` or `refactor plan`: each reads a kill switch, loads config.toml, opens decision ports or consumes a required leading positional before `fs.Parse`. Their help prints a hand-kept flag list instead, and `fathom version` (which reads `--json` by hand, with no FlagSet) lists no flags at all
- **Evidence:** h1 help-flags, 2026-10-09: `TestVerbHelpParseFirstProof` reads 161 handlers parse-first and names, for each of the other 12, the first statement that rules delegation out (for example `edithook.go: if fathomChannelsDisabled()`, `planscontext.go: Parse is handed args[1:]`)
- **Root cause:** each handler builds its FlagSet inline after its own pre-work; no handler exposes a flag-definition step help could call alone
- **Fix:** none yet beyond the pinned lists: `TestVerbHelpStaticListsPinned` fails when a flag is added, renamed or reworded in source without its list. S2's cli-kit move, which declares flags per command, removes the lists; until then a handler can drop its entry by moving its pre-work after Parse where that is behaviour-neutral (verify, init and prime did here)
- **Status:** open (cli-kit-adoption S2)
- **Impact:** twelve help texts are maintained by hand; a default the source computes from config (serve-http's store, addr, call log) is described, not shown
