### O1415 — a licence header longer than 1 KiB left every AFNetworking `.m` ambiguous

- **Symptom:** `indexbench lang-oracle` on AFNetworking read "49 files skipped by ingest's gates (ambiguous)" and no definitions: the `.m` extension is MATLAB's and Objective-C's, and the sniff saw only the first 1 KiB.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `connector.ClassifyDialect` (`sniffScanBytes = 1 << 10`), the `objective-c` manifest's sniff `^\s*(@(interface|protocol|implementation|class|import)\b|#import\b)`
  - **Expected:** the 49 `.m` files are Objective-C
  - **Actual:** each opens with a 20-line MIT licence block, so the first `#import` is past byte 1024 and neither sniff matches
  - **Repro:** `ClassifyDialect(rules, "AFNetworking/AFHTTPSessionManager.m", content)` before `scan_bytes`
- **Root cause:** the window is a constant of the classifier; a language whose files open with a long licence needs a longer one, and a constant raised for everyone would widen every other dialect's sniff.
- **Fix:** `match.sniff.scan_bytes` (schema, manifest, `DialectSniffRule.ScanBytes`): the objective-c manifest reads 16 KiB, every other dialect keeps 1 KiB (`TestClassifyDialectSniffWindow`).
- **Status:** fixed
