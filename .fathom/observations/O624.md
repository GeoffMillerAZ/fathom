### O624 — a literal emitter grep cannot tell an emitter from a consumer: the census' E is an upper bound

- **Symptom:** kube-prometheus, a repository with no service code, reads two E names (`up`, `prometheus_remote_storage_samples_in_total`)
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `calibration/telemetry-census` over kube-prometheus v0.19.0 (`docs/bench/readings/telemetry/census-c0.json`)
  - **Actual:** both literals are queries in `tests/e2e/*.go`, which consume the metrics; thanos' E (80 of 118) likewise counts its own test queries beside its emitters
  - **Repro:** `grep -rn '"up"' tests/e2e` in the checkout
- **Root cause:** TL-6's oracle is lexical by design (a string literal of source code), with no call-site or registration shape
- **Fix:** none needed for the gate: the verdict stands on the upper bound (14.5% < 25%; every Thanos and every unexplained demo name as E still reads 24.4%, census-c0.md); a Stage B reading that must clear 25% would need registration-shaped evidence (a client library's constructor) instead
- **Status:** open (disclosed in census-c0.md)
- **Impact:** E is reported as an upper bound
