### O1134 — the stanza promises matched invariants on every edit; 15 of the 66 invariants can reach an edit

- **Symptom:** this repository's `AGENTS.md` stanza (line 103) says "Obligations — governing rules for the file you are about to touch, and where `docs/intent/` exists its matched invariants — are injected on every edit". An agent reads that as: every invariant that governs the file arrives at the edit. Only the invariants named in `.fathom/guard.cue`'s 15 hand-pasted intent entries can; the other 51 never do.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** the stanza (`obligationsWithIntent`, chosen by `stanzaHookSentences` in `cmd/fathom/init.go` when `intentChannelWired` holds)
  - **Expected:** the stanza names what is delivered (vision 25 §2.2 rule 3, which `stanzaHookSentences` cites: the text never promises a channel it lacks; EP-22).
  - **Actual:** `intentChannelWired` turns true once any guard entry carries intent provenance, so one entry switches on a sentence that speaks for all 66 invariants; the obligations corpus excludes `docs/intent` by design (`TestObligationsCorpusNeverWidensToIntent`), so guard notes are the only channel. Hook injections cite `docs/intent` 1.8% of the time (intent-practice-v2 research `wf_888f8f2d-57b`).
  - **Repro:** `grep -c 'note: *"intent:' .fathom/guard.cue` prints 15; `grep -c '^| I[0-9]' docs/intent/invariants.md` prints 66 (2026-10-17).
- **Root cause:** the EP-22 predicate asks "is the channel wired at all", not "what does it cover", and the sentence was written for full coverage.
- **Fix (proposed shape):** `docs/plans/intent-v2.md` IV-4 (the sentence names what is delivered, the stanza region regenerated and its digests re-recorded), then IV-3a to IV-3c (guard entries generated from the router's surface table, ids plus rung at the edit, repo-wide invariants at session start).
