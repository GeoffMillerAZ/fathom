### O1500 — An ingest emitting about 150,000 call rows failed the whole service with "database or disk is full (13)" while 20 GiB were free, and every observation of the run was lost (issue #8)

- **Symptom:** An ingest emitting about 150,000 call rows failed the whole service with "database or disk is full (13)" while 20 GiB were free, and every observation of the run was lost (issue #8)
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** internal/store ingest (SQLite statement journal)
  - **Expected:** Large row counts ingest, or the failure names the file and the cause
  - **Actual:** The 4 MiB statement-journal spill let the in-memory journal flush in one write of 131,072 bytes or more; SQLite unix VFS seekAndWriteFd masks write lengths with 0x1ffff, so the short write surfaced as SQLITE_FULL. Fixed: the spill is now at or under 0x1ffff (internal/store/stmtspill.go)
