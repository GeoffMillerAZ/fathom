### O342 — a comment-headed nginx .conf is left ambiguous

- **Symptom:** a comment-headed nginx .conf is left ambiguous
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `connector.ClassifyDialect` (the nginx manifest's `.conf` sniff)
  - **Expected:** h5bp/server-configs-nginx's snippet files (`h5bp/security/*.conf`, `h5bp/tls/*.conf`) classify as nginx
  - **Actual:** 12 of its 38 `.conf` files open with more than a KiB of `#` comments, the sniff reads the first KiB only, so they are counted ambiguous and never extracted (disclosed; their 25 includes and 12 file entities are out of the reading's scope)
  - **Repro:** `go run ./cmd/indexbench lang-oracle -dialect nginx -subject workspace/calibration/nginx/server-configs-nginx ...` notes "12 files skipped by ingest's gates (ambiguous)"
- **Root cause:** the sniff window (`sniffScanBytes`, 1 KiB) is shared by every dialect; a `.conf` is claimed only by content because supervisord, redis and php-fpm use the extension too
- **Fix:** none here; a manifest option to skip leading comment lines before the window, or classification by include graph
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** an nginx repository of commented snippets is partly unindexed, disclosed as ambiguous
