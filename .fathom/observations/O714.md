### O714 — the 24 h ring could only bound six of RT-M1's seven days

- **Symptom:** the 2026-10-09 RT-M1 reading had one day of samples; days 1-6 were bounded by the ledger and the marks, never read
- **Evidence:** `internal/tenancy/ring.go` keeps 1,440 minute slots (a sample lands in its minute-of-day slot, so the next day overwrites it); `fathom resources --since` is capped at 24 h; the RT-M1 criteria (max footprint vs exit mark and budget, refuse minutes/day, exit rungs, unclean exits) need seven days
- **Root cause:** the instrument was sized for incident forensics (§13.1), and RT-M1 was pre-registered against it without a seven-day store
- **Fix:** RT-184 rolls a per-UTC-day summary on the existing sampler tick into `resources.days` beside the ring (8 rows); RT-185 `fathom resources --days N` prints it with one RT-M1 verdict (`--json`: `days`, `verdict.state`). No table, no job
- **Status:** fixed in code (h1-rtm1); RT-M1 itself stays open until a real seven-day reading after deploy
- **Impact:** the operator reading RT-M1
