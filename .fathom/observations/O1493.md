### O1493 — Withholding a superseded record's claims can drop a restatement no in-force claim carries

- **Symptom:** RS-M3 withholds `ADR.md#L241-L243` (ADR-013, superseded by ADR-066): "use native pure-Go parsers for the Track A config/IaC formats". ADR-004's tagged claims still serve HCL, k8s YAML and CUE, but proto, gqlparser, goldmark and go-toml are now named by no served claim.
- **Evidence:** `docs/bench/readings/rule-selection/in-force-check.md`, claim 10.
- **Root cause:** a superseded record restated a rule that still binds; d3's status-line oracle withholds the restatement with the record (by design, DM2).
- **Fix:** a recompile from a record in force carries the rule; not a serving change.
- **Status:** open.
- **Impact:** small: one claim of 526, and the rule follows from ADR-066's pure-Go decision.
