### O1297 — EVX-9 verifies by structure once EVX-7 wires `cmdExportReceipt`, though its contract is not built

- **Symptom:** EVX-7's `reachable_from` is `cmdExportReceipt`, which is EVX-9's own symbol and did not exist. Wiring EVX-7 needed a minimal `fathom export receipt` (symbol_location and callers only). `verify --strict` now prints `✓ [EVX-9]` for the unclaimed row, while its contract (the references and certification kinds, `--require-clean`, the 256 KB median and the 2 s warm bound) is not built.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** bin/fathom verify --strict --plan docs/plans/portable-receipts.md (scratch ingest of evx1-rcpt HEAD)
  - **Expected:** a row whose prose names behavior the code lacks does not read as provable
  - **Actual:** the `{symbol, package, reachable_from}` contract holds, so EVX-9 reads verified-if-claimed
  - **Repro:** the verify line for EVX-9 on this branch
- **Root cause:** a symbol contract proves the symbol exists and is reached, not the flags and bars the row's text names; EVX-7 borrowed a later row's symbol as its entry point.
- **Fix:** EVX-9's `reachable_from` is now `TestExportReceiptAllKindsRequireCleanAndBars`, a test that does not exist yet, so a claim on the row cannot verify until the contract (the remaining kinds, `--require-clean`, the 256 KB and 2 s bars) is built with that test. The box stays `[ ]`.
- **Status:** resolved
