### O293 — SV-M1 read CUT, but `fathom plans lines` still prints the claims-rows and touches-rows classes the reading killed

- **Symptom:** SV-M1 read CUT, but `fathom plans lines` still prints the claims-rows and touches-rows classes the reading killed
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom plans lines --json` over the rebuilt 2026-09-29 repositories (docs/bench/readings/stewardship/lines-replay.sh, lines-replay-score.py), wave cd branch cd-steward
  - **Expected:** at most 25 lines flagged for a look (claims rows, touches rows or at risk) across fathom and agentic-kit, the bar fixed in SV-M1
  - **Actual:** 36 flagged (fathom 13, agentic-kit 23; 40 with the three held clones); 18 kit lines read touches rows because their diffs name `New` under `foundations/agent/mcp-client` (row `handoff-h7-patterns-bench.md#md-001`), though a third to a half of their commits are patch-equivalent on main and most of their files read the same there; landed read 46 of 46 on the consolidation's clean record and every held item read at risk
  - **Repro:** lines-replay.sh <read-only copy of workspace/durable-2026-09-29/cleanup> <scratch> <fathom binary> <fathom clone>, then lines-replay-score.py <scratch> <copy>
- **Root cause:** the lexical tier matches a generic symbol under a package, and the landed test (ancestor, patch-id, content-equal) cannot see work that landed and was then edited on main, so superseded lines read as touching open rows
- **Fix:** TODO: ADR-060's revisit trigger fired ("SV-M1 reads CUT: remove what the reading killed"). Removing the two classes changes the `plans lines --json` shape and SV-1's row text, so it waits for the owner; SV-3 is refused in the same change as the reading. A per-commit "already applied" landed test (`git apply --reverse --check` of each unmatched commit) is the candidate a new reading would have to measure
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** an operator reading `plans lines` sees 36 lines to look at where the bar allows 25; the landed and at-risk classes stand
