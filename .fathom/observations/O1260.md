### O1260 — the first test-reach call after a graph build takes about 9 s on fathom, past every edit-hook deadline

- **Symptom:** in each of three scratch daemon starts, the first `blast_radius` call took 8.4 to 9.6 s. Later calls answered in 24 to 39 ms from the graph memoized under the store watermark. A re-ingest, or the 30-minute cache idle drop, makes the next call cold again. The readouts plan's Start lists RO-M13 among readings that "make no daemon call", but RO-M13 runs through the daemon. Its answer depends on the budget: 0 of 50 edits answered at the unmanaged 512 MiB default (O1073) and 50 of 50 at the managed 4 GiB auto budget.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** fathom serve-http (scratch, unmanaged) + `blast_radius` as `fetchBlastRadius` sends it
  - **Expected:** the edit hook's test-reach call fits its deadline, or names the miss
  - **Actual:** warm-up calls of 9,578, 8,372 and 9,535 ms, against `blastRadiusTimeout` of 500 ms and RO-M13's bar of 2 s
  - **Repro:** docs/bench/readings/readouts/seam-tests-select-speed.md (warm-up column)
- **Root cause:** a cold build of fathom's call graph takes about 9 s. The memo lives only until the watermark next moves.
