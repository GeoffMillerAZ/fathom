### O1014 — On this repository's index the default daemon rebuilds the call graph on every graph-backed call

- **Symptom:** a scratch serve-http (default `[tenancy]`, no config) over a scratch store of this repository answers `localize`, `blast_radius` and `test_impact` in 8 to 19 s per call, the second call as slow as the first; `obligations`, `find_symbol` and `query_symbols` answer in 10 to 250 ms.
- **Evidence:** EW-75 controlled run; a CPU profile of a warm `localize` (budget 1, one find_symbol hop) spent 9.8 of 10.8 s in `callgraph.Build` under `memo.GraphFor`; the daemon's stderr: `tenancy: normal → shed: footprint+children 504.0 MiB ≥ shed mark 384 MiB`. A caller that leaves before half a build's expected cost cancels the flight (`memo.Group.leave`), so a hook's abandoned call never warms the graph for the next caller.
- **Root cause:** the call graph of a 53k-symbol service does not stay resident under the default cache budget at shed; every graph-backed call pays the build.
- **Fix:** RT territory, after RT-M1 reads (the table and cache freeze): either a budget that holds the largest service's graphs, or a graph-backed hook-origin call refused at admission when the graph is not resident and its deadline is shorter than a build.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** no hook deadline holds a graph-backed call on this index (EW-76's FAIL rows); in the fleet's call log, agent `symbol_context` calls were abandoned 325 of 1,827 times, plausibly the same cause (unread).
