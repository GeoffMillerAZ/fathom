### O1014 — On this repository's index the default daemon rebuilds the call graph on every graph-backed call

- **Symptom:** a scratch serve-http (default `[tenancy]`, no config) over a scratch store of this repository answers `localize`, `blast_radius` and `test_impact` in 8 to 19 s per call, the second call as slow as the first; `obligations`, `find_symbol` and `query_symbols` answer in 10 to 250 ms.
- **Evidence:** EW-75 controlled run; a CPU profile of a warm `localize` (budget 1, one find_symbol hop) spent 9.8 of 10.8 s in `callgraph.Build` under `memo.GraphFor`; the daemon's stderr: `tenancy: normal → shed: footprint+children 504.0 MiB ≥ shed mark 384 MiB`. A caller that leaves before half a build's expected cost cancels the flight (`memo.Group.leave`), so a hook's abandoned call never warms the graph for the next caller.
  - **verify_plan, 2026-10-17 (wave fx5, O1161):** the setup was a fresh scratch ingest of 78157704 and a scratch unmanaged serve-http with a 512 MiB budget (shed after the first call, refuse 25 to 50 s in). Through it, `fathom verify --plan docs/plans/language-breadth.md` (768 rows) took 54 verify_plan calls and 665 s wall clock (643 s server time). Each call answered about 13 rows, because the 32 KB dispatch budget cut the rest, and took 8.5 to 27 s. The same binary over the same store in `--store` mode took 8.6 to 10.7 s, three runs each of v0.15.2 and v0.15.3. A v0.15.2 serve-http was the same: 8.6 to 25 s per call, and it stopped at call 11 on the refuse-level building partial. So v0.15.3 did not regress. CPU profile of the daemon's first 30 s: `callgraph.Build` under `memo.GraphFor` (`BuildBoth`, `resolveCtorScoped` 15.0 s) took 16.3 of 26.7 s (61%), and the rows (`verify.verifyTask`) took 6.4 s (24%). After the CLI stopped re-verifying rows it could not receive (O1161), `callgraph.Build` took 20.3 of 26.9 s (75%), the rows 3.4 s (13%), and per-call latency stayed 8.5 to 13.7 s. Every follow-up call rebuilds the graph. The run then ended on `the call graph for service fathom could not be loaded: context canceled; fathom is at tenancy level refuse`.
  - **one call per plan, 2026-10-17 (wave fx6, O1169):** a fresh scratch ingest of ab36d461 and a scratch unmanaged serve-http with the deployed daemon's budget, 4 GiB (`FATHOM_TENANCY_BUDGET`; the managed daemon's `/statusz` reads 4294967296 and `~/.fathom/config.toml` has no `[tenancy]`). The 32 KB per call is the 16,000-byte dispatch budget sent twice, as the text block and as structuredContent. `verify --plan docs/plans/language-breadth.md`: origin/main 63 calls, 12.7 s; ab36d461 1 call, 7.7 s, the same verdicts, the answer 1,768,157 bytes on the wire. `plans status --service fathom`: origin/main 559 verify_plan calls, 17.7 s, exit 0, no plan unmeasured; ab36d461 76 calls (one per plan), 13.1 s, exit 0, no plan unmeasured. The ladder stayed at normal in both runs: at the deployed budget the graph stays resident and memory pressure does not dominate. At fx5's 512 MiB, ab36d461's verify took 1 call and 7.5 s (fx5: 54 calls, 665 s), but `plans status` reached shed and then refuse: 318.8 s, exit 2, 1 of 76 plans unmeasured (`claude-code-mods.md`: `product reach: context canceled; fathom is at tenancy level refuse`). Tenancy unchanged.
- **Root cause:** the call graph of a 53k-symbol service does not stay resident under the default cache budget at shed; every graph-backed call pays the build.
- **Fix:** RT territory, after RT-M1 reads (the table and cache freeze): either a budget that holds the largest service's graphs, or a graph-backed hook-origin call refused at admission when the graph is not resident and its deadline is shorter than a build.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** no hook deadline holds a graph-backed call on this index (EW-76's FAIL rows); in the fleet's call log, agent `symbol_context` calls were abandoned 325 of 1,827 times, plausibly the same cause (unread).
