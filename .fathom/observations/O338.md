### O338 — istioctl analyze --use-kube=false skips its destination-host analyzer: IST0101 "Referenced host not found" is unavailable offline

- **Symptom:** istioctl analyze --use-kube=false skips its destination-host analyzer: IST0101 "Referenced host not found" is unavailable offline
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** istioctl 1.31.1 (osx-arm64 release, sha256 3f37edda…) `analyze --use-kube=false -A --verbose`
  - **Expected:** the wave px brief: a tier-A Istio cross-check from IST0101 ReferencedResourceNotFound naming a destination host that resolves to nothing
  - **Actual:** a VirtualService whose destination is `missing` (no Service, no ServiceEntry) reports nothing; `--verbose` lists virtualservice.DestinationHostAnalyzer under "Skipped analyzers"; without `-A` nothing outside namespace default is analyzed at all
  - **Repro:** `istioctl analyze --use-kube=false -A --verbose <file with a VirtualService routing to host: missing>`
- **Root cause:** offline (file) analysis does not load the cluster's Services, and the analyzer's inputs include them
- **Fix:** the kubernetes oracle's cross-check uses what runs offline: virtualservice.DestinationRuleAnalyzer's IST0101 host+subset, which applies Istio's host normalization to destinations and DestinationRule hosts (30 of 30 agree on bookinfo)
- **Status:** worked around; LB-F99-rest keeps the tier-C ceiling until a runtime oracle (a kind cluster) reads the routes
- **Impact:** Kubernetes route pairs stay at L3 good, not excellent
