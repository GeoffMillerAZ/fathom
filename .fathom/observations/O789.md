### O789 — file-resolved edges from files that share one SymbolKey keep duplicate rows, and which duplicates survive depends on every other edge

- **Symptom:** D1's cost reading (O787) found zod's non-module typed edge rows 5,823 at origin/main and 5,819 with the lever on, though D1 removes only edges to a module symbol: the distinct (from_file, from_symbol, to_symbol, kind) sets are equal (5,810 each); the extra rows are duplicates.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `sqlite3` on the scratch stores of O787 (`select ... from typed_edges where from_symbol like '%resolution/src/index%'`).
  - zod's packages/resolution/src/index.ts, index.mts and index.cts share the key `packages/resolution/src/index` (an extension is no part of a package); the store holds, for example, `index.<module> -> classic/schemas.string` twice from index.ts at origin/main and once with the lever on.
  - `sortEdges` (internal/fusion/typedcalls.go) orders by FromSymbol, ToSymbol, Kind, not FromFile, with an unstable sort; resolveImports' CompactFunc compares FromFile too, so it removes a duplicate only when the unstable order leaves the two adjacent.
- **Root cause:** the comparator and the compaction disagree on what one edge is.
- **Fix:** proposed, not this item's file: sort by FromFile as well (or compact by a set), so a run's rows are the distinct edges whatever else changed.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a handful of duplicate rows per shared key (13 on zod); a store-size comparison between two commits can move by rows no change made.
