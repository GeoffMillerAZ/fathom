### O841 — HTML's L3 has no denominator on the queue's calibration subjects: no measured page links to another

- **Symptom:** html.json, re-read in wave lf5 under W1 and K19's pair key (LB-F441, LB-F447), reads L1 and L2 excellent and "L3 not met: import_resolution not measured", while the holdout reads L3 excellent (LB-F448).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect html` at a5182ffd with the parse5 and lychee tool oracles (calibration/oracles/parse5, calibration/oracles/lychee) on bootstrap v5.3.8 `js/tests/visual` and html5-boilerplate v9.0.1.
  - **Actual:** lychee 0.24.2 `--offline` resolves 76 local links on bootstrap's 13 pages: 63 are a page's own fragment (`href="#"`, `href="#collapseOne"`, the page itself) and 13 leave the subpath (`../../../dist/css/bootstrap.min.css`); html5-boilerplate's pages link to a stylesheet, an icon and a manifest. No link joins two measured `.html`/`.htm` files, so import_resolution and the file edges count 0 of 0.
  - **Repro:** the README commands of both oracles, then lang-oracle with `-unmatched`.
- **Root cause:** L3's unit for HTML is a link between two measured pages (design §1.8: a script, stylesheet or image target is out of scope, as the html resolver's universe is the html files), and the queue chose its subjects for ids and forms before HTML had an L3 oracle.
- **Fix:** none here (the subjects are the queue's). LB-Q-html-rest names the trigger: re-plan the subjects to pages that link to one another, or an owner ruling that a holdout's reading meets the row.
- **Status:** closed (wave lf6, owner ruling M4): startbootstrap-sb-admin-2 v4.1.4 joined html.json (LB-F506, O925) and the re-read reads L3 excellent (LB-F507).
- **Impact:** LB-Q-html stays open although the dialect's links resolve (holdout: 993 of 993 in-repo links, file_edge_precision .9874, file_edge_recall 1).
