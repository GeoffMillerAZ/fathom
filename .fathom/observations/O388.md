### O388 — a ConstraintTemplate declared in several files resolves no constraint: gatekeeper-library's artifacthub tree republishes every template per version

- **Symptom:** on the whole gatekeeper-library checkout a `constraint_template` service-name reference is ambiguous wherever its kind is declared in library/ and again per artifacthub/<version>/, so that constraint draws no template edge
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fusion.ResolveDocXrefs` (service-name: the one entity of that name in another file)
  - **Expected:** a constraint joins the template its suite pairs it with
  - **Actual:** a template kind republished under artifacthub/ is declared more than once in the checkout (K8sRequiredLabels: 6 template.yaml files), so service-name refuses its constraints' join; on the `library` subpath (one declaration per kind) all 199 cross-references agree with gator verify
  - **Repro:** `find . -name template.yaml | xargs grep -h 'kind: K8sRequiredLabels' | wc -l` in gatekeeper-library d2b86f87 (6)
- **Root cause:** service-name refuses a name declared twice (never a guess); a nearest-declaration rule (route-ref's) is not doc-xref's
- **Fix:** none; the reading is taken on `subpath: library` and the gatekeeper PROVENANCE discloses it
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a repository that vendors a template in more than one place draws no constraint-to-template edge
