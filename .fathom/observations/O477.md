### O477 — a package patch with no new typed edges dropped the package's typed edges from the cached graph

- **Symptom:** `ReplacePackage(pkg, nodes, nil)` documents nil as "leave typed edges alone" (SU-5's one-file refresh, `memo.PatchPackage` from `/refresh-file`), but the patched graph had none of pkg's typed out-edges until the next ingest rebuilt it
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `go test ./internal/analysis/callgraph -run TestServedCalls_ReplacePackageEqualsFresh` (wave lg), comparing every caller, callee, reference and test-impact answer of the patched graph with a fresh `BuildBoth`
  - **Expected:** the patched graph answers as a fresh `BuildBoth` over the same nodes and typed edges
  - **Actual (before the fix):** pkg's typed edges (direct calls, dispatch fan-outs, function values) were stripped with its syntax edges ("they share the same adjacency maps") and only the syntax edges were re-derived; with E (LB-F199) the re-derived method edges from the typed file would then have gone too
  - **Repro:** BuildBoth over the servedcalls fixture, then `ReplacePackage("app", appNodes, nil)`: `Compare -> Store.Equal` lost its `direct` kind
- **Root cause:** `ReplacePackage` stripped every outgoing edge of pkg's old nodes, typed included, and re-added typed edges only from a non-nil argument
- **Fix:** LB-F202: with nil typed edges, pkg's current typed out-edges are read before the strip and added back through `AddTypedEdges` after the syntax edges are re-derived; E is applied to the re-derived edges either way
- **Status:** closed 2026-10-02 (LB-F202)
- **Impact:** a one-file refresh no longer degrades the refreshed package's callers, blast radius and verify reach to syntax evidence until the next ingest
