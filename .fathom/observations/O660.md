### O660 — the renderer dropped wrapped goal titles from INTENT.md

- **Symptom:** `fathom intent render --playbook` listed 7 goals and 8 questions where scope.md carries more; G12 (scope.md L220) and G16 (L67-68) were absent, 2 of 27 goals
- **Evidence:** cmd/fathom/intent_playbook.go and INTENT.md at 9e66f323
- **Root cause:** `boldLeadRe` matched one line at a time, so a bold lead whose closing `**` sits on a later line never matched
- **Fix:** `boldLeads` joins the lead's paragraph before finding the closing `**` (HP-37); a golden fixture covers single-line, wrapped and no-trailing-period leads
- **Status:** fixed by the K13 commit
- **Impact:** every reader of INTENT.md saw a partial goal list that looked complete
