### O1438 — global-name (LB-F580) shipped; its first cut stored same-file edges past the cost gate

- **Symptom:** the first global-name cut (b54ccdae) bound a GraphQL or Prisma file's references to its own definitions too (the file resolver's same-file binding), and the ingest cost gate read +31.5% store on octokit/graphql-schema (one 1.2 MB file, 10,439 definitions, every reference same-file) and +20.8% on vendure, over K26's +20%.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `w-f580/scripts/costgate.py` (median of 5 scratch ingests, alternating with origin/main's binary at 441b225e): octokit/graphql-schema store 7,168,000 to 9,428,992 bytes, vendure 4,489,216 to 5,423,104
  - **Tool:** after 999d94bc (no same-file edge under global-name): +3.1% octokit, +3.6% gqlgen, +14.1% vendure, +0.6% cal.com, +6.6% dub, +6.9% papermark store; wall at most +9.0% (sub-second ingests)
- **Root cause:** `callEdges` binds a call to the file's own top-level name (`localTop`) for every strategy. For a schema that is the union of its files, those edges have no file-granularity value (the xref score never counts them) and their rows dominate the store.
- **Fix:** 999d94bc: under global-name `localTop` is nil, so the importer never edges to itself (the rule LB-F580 states); the L3 readings (`docs/bench/readings/lang/graphql.json`, `prisma.json`) are unchanged by it, 724 of 724 and 303 of 303.
- **Status:** fixed
