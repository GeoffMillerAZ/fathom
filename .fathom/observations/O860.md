### O860 — TypeScript misses L4 on its TSX holdout (react-bootstrap): JSX elements are calls in the dialect and no call site in the truth

- **Symptom:** the TSX holdout pinned with ruling L5 (b17f903c), read once, reads level_met L1: L1 good, L2 unmet, L3 and L4 excellent. LB-Q-typescript, proven by fp-ts + rxjs (no .tsx), unflips.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect typescript -scip-defs -only src/**,test/**,tests/**` at 753ca697 (two passes, D3 claims), against scip-typescript 0.4.0 over the same globs under the subject's tsconfig.json (extends @4c/tsconfig/web.json: strict, noImplicitAny, jsx react) with `yarn install --frozen-lockfile --ignore-scripts` (yarn 1.22.19) in a second checkout (`docs/bench/readings/lang/holdout/typescript-react-bootstrap.json` since wave lf6, when the next TSX holdout took `holdout/typescript.json`).
  - **Metrics (233 files, 216 .tsx, now in TypeScript's measured scope):** L1 sym_recall 1 (279 of 279), sym_precision .9362 (26 D3 admissions); L2 call_recall .9925 (1,710 of 1,723), call_precision .7844 (1,710 of 2,180; 50 oracle-blind pairs left out, 2.2%); L3 import_resolution 1 (751 of 751), file_edge_precision 1, file_edge_recall .8744; L4 def_mapping .9964 (1,115 of 1,119; 869 object-literal properties out, D2; none out of scope), typed_recall .9939 (6,363 of 6,402).
  - **L2 residual:** 443 of the 470 fathom-only pairs are JSX elements: the tsx variant records `<Button …>` and `<Ctx.Provider>` as a call to the tag (LB-F377), and the truth's call sites are references written `name(` (LB-F174), which a JSX element never is; without them call_precision would read .9845. The other 27: 8 calls of `forwardRef` by its own name, and 19 pairs whose caller the truth names otherwise, mostly a call in a function nested in a component that the truth gives to the component (O443's enclosure: Modal's updateDialogStyle, whose counterparts are among the 13 oracle-only pairs).
  - **L1 residual:** 18 of the 19 unadmitted dialect-only definitions are components bound to a wrapper call (`const Popover = React.forwardRef<…>(`), which scip-typescript kinds a variable (outside L1's intersection) and D3's arrow-const class admits only when the `=>` is within three lines of the binding; the 19th is an arrow const whose `=>` is further down.
- **Root cause:** a matching question no rule settles: whether a JSX element is a call site in TypeScript's L2 (the React reading grades render pairs on its own, react.json) and whether a wrapper-bound component is a definition the scip oracle misses.
- **Fix:** none here (read once, K19). Trigger on LB-Q-typescript-rest.
- **Status:** open (owner decision)
- **Impact:** TypeScript's L4 holds on .ts libraries (fp-ts + rxjs) and not on TSX-heavy code, by L2's matching rule only: L4 itself reads excellent there.
