### O984 — No Angular template grammar parses the calibration templates under the 5% line

- **Symptom:** the Angular template dialect (LB-Q-angular) cannot be built: dlvandenberg/tree-sitter-angular v0.9.2, the only maintained Angular grammar with a licence, leaves an ERROR or MISSING node in 13 of the 72 templates the Angular compiler reads on the two calibration subjects (18.1%), over design §14.2's 5% line.
- **Evidence:** `docs/bench/readings/lang/angular-grammar.md` (wave lf7, item angularastro): realworld build-50 1 of 10 (`*rxLet="tags$; let tags"`), angular/components v22.2.2 `src/material` 12 of 62: a unary minus in a conditional branch (`? -1 : tabIndex`, nine files), a non-null assertion (`_todayDate()!`, five), a `@for` header's trailing semicolon (one). The default branch's head differs from the tag only in `queries/highlights.scm`. tree-sitter-html, which reads these templates today as `.html`, leaves 6 of the 72 in error (8.3%).
- **Root cause:** the grammar's Angular expression rules lack the unary minus after `?`/`:`, the postfix `!`, a structural directive's microsyntax with `let`, and an empty `@for` clause after `track`.
- **Fix:** none here: LB-Q-angular-rest is refused with the measurement; templates stay the html dialect's. Upstream issues would be the route (not filed: no network writes from a wave).
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** no Angular selector or pipe use is extracted from a template; `.component.ts` classes stay TypeScript's. Trigger: a tree-sitter-angular release parsing both subjects at their pins (`calibration/corpus.d/angular.yaml`) with at most 5% of templates in error.
