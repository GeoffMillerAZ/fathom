### O1105 — `obligations` served a compiled adr-rule from a section the same response reports stale

- **Symptom:** `obligations` for go.mod served `go.mod carries replace github.com/geoffmilleraz/agentic-kit => ./third_party/agentic-kit.` (ADR.md#L650-L651, doc-claimed) although ADR-070 amended ADR-019 and layer_coverage.uncompiled_reasons listed that section as `stale`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** MCP obligations (renderObligationReceipt)
  - **Expected:** a rule whose source section changed since the compile is not served as current (I38)
  - **Actual:** the claim rode at doc-claimed tier next to its own stale reason; Obligation carried no subject to tie them
- **Root cause:** the serve path attached layer_coverage after rendering rules and never cross-checked the two.
- **Fix:** Obligation.Subject (not serialized); the receipt reads coverage first, withholds claims whose subject is `stale`, counts them in `layer_coverage.stale_withheld`, and the raw fallback (RawFallbackRules, restricted to those subjects) serves the section's current text. corpus_grew/budget/failed are untouched.
- **Status:** fixed
