### O299 — O128 recurs on compile_layer: 424 s, logged ok, 0 bytes answered

- **Symptom:** compile_layer over /rpc ran 424 s, logged ok, and the client got an empty body, although curl allowed 1800 s.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** compile_layer over /rpc (ClassIngest, internal/mcp/costclass.go)
  - **Expected:** the ingest-class detach hook lifts the route's 150 s write deadline.
  - **Actual:** the hook's `http.NewResponseController(w).SetWriteDeadline(time.Time{})` returned ErrNotSupported (ignored): `w` was loggingMiddleware's `*loggingResponseWriter`, which had Flush but no Unwrap. The deadline stayed and cut the answer off.
  - **Repro:** TestCompileLayerLongRunNeverAnswersEmptyBody (newDaemonServer with a 300 ms injected write deadline over newHTTPHandlerLogged, a fake compile of 900 ms): `Post ...: EOF` before the fix.
- **Root cause:** a ResponseWriter wrapper without Unwrap in the production chain. Not the batch or extended surface, not IdleTimeout, not a stale binary: every ingest-class call through the logging wrapper was affected (EW-18's ingest answer-with-queue-state path masked it for `ingest`).
- **Fix:** EW-36: `loggingResponseWriter.Unwrap`.
- **Status:** fixed — EW-36
- **Impact:** a long compile's answer (run id, cost) was lost though the work completed.
