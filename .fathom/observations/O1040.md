### O1040 — The Helm file:// grading first took dependencies from the walk, which reads Chart.yaml alone

- **Symptom:** the first run of `helm-oracle -file-deps-out` (7b1ad853, never committed as a reading) graded 2 file:// edges on linkerd/linkerd2 version-2.20 while the same run's `helm show chart` cross-check reported 5 mismatched charts.
- **Evidence:** the cross-check's lines: `charts/linkerd2-cni`, `charts/patch`, `multicluster/charts/linkerd-multicluster`, `multicluster/charts/linkerd-multicluster-link` and `viz/charts/linkerd-viz` are apiVersion v1 charts whose `requirements.yaml` names `partials` by a file:// path; Helm's loader reads it into the chart's dependencies, the walk (main.go walkChart) does not.
- **Root cause:** filedeps.go iterated the walk's dependency list, so the oracle was the walk's reading of the chart, not Helm's.
- **Fix:** cc00f679: every chart is listed by `helm dependency list` and its file:// rows graded (TestFileDepsHelm gains a v1 chart with requirements.yaml). The correction only adds truth, against the dialect, so it was no tuning toward a pass; helm.json was read once after it (fea3c57c).
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** linkerd2's truth is 7 file:// edges, not 2; the pre-registered criterion is judged on 13 edges, not 8 (O1041).
