### O1370 — the waves guide never named the integrator's epoch bump it was to replace

- **Symptom:** AP-15 asks `docs/guides/agent-waves.md` to replace "the integrator's single epoch bump" with a bump file per wave, but the guide's integrator steps did not mention the epoch at all; the rule lived only in ADR-048 decision D2 and the worker recipe (`docs/design/language-breadth.md` section 13, step 24).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `grep -n "epoch\|ExtractorEpoch" docs/guides/agent-waves.md` at c881eeac: no match
- **Root cause:** the integrator's renumbering was carried by briefs and the ADR, not by the guide.
- **Fix:** the guide's gate section now names `task merge-drivers`, `task regen` and the bump file `internal/fusion/epochs/<wave>.md`; the recipe's step 24 names the bump file.
- **Status:** fixed
