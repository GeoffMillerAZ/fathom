- **Root cause and verdicts:** (1) oracle environment, b. and most of a., c., d. and j.: the index was taken with nest's `paths` and zod's self-references dropped and no dependency installed (O441, fixed in the recipe); (2) oracle vocabulary, h., the accessor reads, the `<constructor>` references and the references that are no call: the harness reads scip-typescript at the call site as written (LB-F174, Python's and Go's rules plus its own names), counted and disclosed; (3) extractor: `new X(...)` unrecorded (LB-F171), uncompacted call text and the non-null `!` (i., LB-F172), and decorators and class fields with no owning definition (LB-F173); (4) what no environment resolves (`super(...)`, a receiver typed any): left out as unjudged (LB-F175, §1.4 amendment 2026-10-02), counted; (5) g1 and g2, callers the oracle does not define as module or member symbols: left as disclosed misses, an owner decision (O443, O265).
- **Fix:** LB-F171..LB-F175 (91dcf2a8, abf7e55d, 43c90bb4). TypeScript call_precision .5186 to .9235 (10,064 of 10,898) and call_recall .8762 to .9836 (10,064 of 10,232), L2 excellent; JavaScript .1919 to .6173 (629 of 1,019) and .8426 to .8897 (629 of 707).
- **Status:** fixed (TypeScript L2); open by design (JavaScript's ceiling, O442; the enclosure rule, O443)
- **Impact:** `fathom callers` and `symbol_context` on TS/JS code now show who constructs a class, who a decorator decorates and what a class field calls; the TS reading's L2 names the extractor for what is the extractor's, and 87% of what remains (726 of 834) is a caller scip-typescript has only as a local.
