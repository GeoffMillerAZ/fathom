### O667 — decide adapters disagree on a score answer and one reports no distribution, read as confidence 1.0 (the DP RESULT stays open, LOW)

- **Symptom:** the decision-port adapters asked the same score question return different scores, and the `llm` adapter's answer carries confidence 1.0, so a reader cannot tell its point answer from a calibrated one
- **Evidence:** the decision-port reading behind the DP RESULT (open, LOW); `third_party/agentic-kit/foundations/decision/types.go` (the Answer doc, lines 112 to 116): for a score question Confidence is computed over the level distribution when the adapter's wire carries one (typesafe, openai), else over a degenerate single outcome, which is 1.0 by Confidence's own k<=1 rule (llm)
- **Root cause:** the score shape lets an adapter with no distribution report the maximum confidence instead of an absent one
- **Fix:** none here (agentic-kit owns the type); a score answer without a distribution should carry an absent confidence with its reason, and the calibrated-band policy (P24) should refuse to rank on it
- **Status:** open (filed only; LOW)
- **Impact:** a consumer ranking by a decide score treats a distribution-less adapter as certain
