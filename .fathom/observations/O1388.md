- **Symptom:** the Less item (LB-Q-less, wave lang5) was unblocked by CSS's committed L1 reading (D6) and its queue grammar jimliang/tree-sitter-less passes the licence check, but it does not parse the queue's subjects.
- **Evidence:** `indexbench lang-pin -tree` on jimliang/tree-sitter-less e5ae6245 (v1.0.0-d plus 3): MIT, basis LICENSE; ships src/parser.c (ABI 14) and an external scanner; built blob 276,666 bytes raw, 79,406 gzip (not locked). Files whose tree holds an ERROR or MISSING node (tree-sitter CLI 0.27.0 `parse`, the harness's LB-F59 query on the same tree): twbs/bootstrap v3.4.1 (68b0d231) 51 of 98, Semantic-Org/Semantic-UI master (597843ab) 46 of 48, together 97 of 146 (66.4%) against the 5% line. The other MIT candidates read at their pin: mdovale/tree-sitter-less 02988c7 (the same grammar.js, 97 of 146), rhino1998/tree-sitter-less 2bd739e (the catalogue's; src/parser.c is ABI 11 and was regenerated with `tree-sitter generate`; bootstrap 38 of 98, Semantic-UI 4 of 48, 42 of 146, 28.8%); iceprosurface/tree-sitter-less ships no grammar.js or parser; codepen/tree-sitter-less carries no licence. The first error per file, by shape: a mixin definition with `;`-separated parameters (`.m(@a: 2px; @b: solid) { }`), a guard (`.m when (@x = y) { }`), a detached ruleset (`@r: { }`), a bare top-level mixin call (`.loadUIOverrides();`, the last statement of every Semantic UI component), `~"..."` escapes.
- **Root cause:** the grammar derives from the CSS grammar and covers Less's rule syntax, not its mixin and detached-ruleset surface, which both subjects use throughout. `lang-probe` truncates its tree at 200 lines, so error counts must come from the CLI or the harness, not the probe's printout.
- **Fix:** none in this wave. §14.2: no grammar that parses real code (over 5% of files in error) is a refusal. LB-Q-less-rest is refused, LB-Q-less keeps its contract and stays open, `.less` stays disclosed as unsupported, no dialect is written, nothing is dispatched and no blob is added to the lock. The subjects are pinned in calibration/corpus.d/less.yaml for the re-measure.
- **Status:** refused; trigger: a tree-sitter-less commit, or another allowlisted Less grammar the queue re-pins, that parses both subjects with at most 5% of files in error. Then the recipe resumes at the dialect (mixin, variable), the tier-C definitions oracle (ctags has no Less parser; the `less` package's parse tree, tier A, would grade definitions) and L3 through `@import` with the relative-path strategy, with `less` render imports as the tier-A truth. ExtractorEpoch need: none (no output changes).
- **Impact:** Less stays unsupported; CSS's L1 reading and the SCSS reading are unaffected.
