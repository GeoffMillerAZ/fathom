### O580 — plans archive absorption expands neither long-form ranges nor letter-digit ids

- **Symptom:** plans archive absorption expands neither long-form ranges nor letter-digit ids
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** fathom plans archive (cmd/fathom/plans_archive.go expandIDNotation, idRangeRe)
  - **Expected:** a CHANGELOG line written `CS-1..CS-7` or `LB-F208..LB-F210` (the long form every current entry uses) absorbs CS-2..CS-6 and LB-F209, as `SH-1..6` absorbs SH-2..5
  - **Actual:** idRangeRe matches only `<PREFIX>-<n>..<m>` with a bare number after the dots, and its prefix must end in `-` before digits, so `CS-1..CS-7` and every `LB-F<n>` range expand to nothing; only the two endpoint ids are absorbed. The 0.10.0 roll kept every token verbatim in docs/changelog/0.10.0.md, so nothing is lost today, but a collapsed line that names only a range absorbs less than it reads
  - **Repro:** go test a call of expandIDNotation("CS-1..CS-7 LB-F208..LB-F210"): it returns ""
