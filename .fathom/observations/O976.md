### O976 — The queue's second Vue subject is no OSI-licensed code

- **Symptom:** vuejs/docs, a calibration subject the vue queue entry names, cannot be pinned (design §13.3: a subject's licence must be OSI-approved).
- **Evidence:** vuejs/docs 40aa88af `LICENSE`: "The contents of this repository, except for all image files, are licensed under CC BY 4.0".
- **Root cause:** the queue's subjects were proposed before their licences were read at a pin.
- **Fix:** LB-F534 substitutes vuejs/vitepress v1.6.4 (MIT, the Vue team's own Vue 3 application, 97 `.vue` files whose default theme imports its components by relative `.vue` paths), saying why in `calibration/corpus.d/vue.yaml`.
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** none on the reading's rules; the queue entry's `calibration_subjects` still names vuejs/docs (an Opus triage re-pins it).
