### O1414 — a selector ends in a colon, which every last-segment name reader cuts at

- **Symptom:** `Shape.initWithFrame:style:` read by `langbench.BareName` is `""` and by `store.CalleeName` is `""`: both take the text after the last `.` or `:`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `internal/langbench/score.go` `BareName` (LastIndexAny `.:#/\ )`), `internal/store/derived.go` `CalleeName` (after the last `:` then the last `.`), used by `store/pointlookup.go` for the callers of a symbol
  - **Expected:** the selector is the symbol's own name
  - **Actual:** a callers lookup of an Objective-C method by its callee key matches nothing in the store's name column for any selector with a keyword; the harness needed `name_match: selector` to compare the part after the owner's `.` whole
  - **Repro:** `store.CalleeName("svc::Shape.initWithFrame:style:")`
- **Root cause:** `:` is the callee key's service separator in the store and a path or scope separator in other languages' names; neither reader knows a selector.
- **Fix:** harness side done (`name_match: "selector"`, `langbench.NameMatchSelector`). Store side open: a callee key's tail should be cut at the last `.` only when the tail after it holds a `:` that is not `::` and is not the service separator (engine row, not a dialect change).
- **Status:** open
