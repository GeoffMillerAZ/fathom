### O704 — K20 needs the wave's one ExtractorEpoch bump: workflow, action, compose, serverless, CloudFormation and collector files extract differently

- **Symptom:** a GitHub Actions workflow or action.yml fingerprinted before K20 keeps a GHARunStep beside each id'd step's GHAStep; a compose file keeps no list-form or OTEL_RESOURCE_ATTRIBUTES identity; a serverless, CloudFormation, compose or collector file keeps an interpolated identity; a collector connector with a namespace keeps its default-named families
- **Evidence:** the extraction diff against 6280cfd4 on the calibration subjects (71 id'd GHARunSteps gone, 7 templated serverless identities gone and their functions' references back) and the changed goldens (github-actions workflow-basic and action-composite, compose, cloudformation and serverless telemetry fixtures, otel-collector gateway and layer)
- **Root cause:** a change of dialect data and engine changes extraction without a content change, so the stat gate would honor a stale fingerprint
- **Fix:** the integrator's one bump of `internal/fusion/extractor_epoch.go` for wave c2 (not edited by this item)
- **Status:** open (until wave c2 integration)
- **Impact:** until the bump, an already-ingested repository keeps its pre-K20 GHA steps and identities; the GHA pin nodes are unchanged (byte-identical to the retired wrapper's)
