### O490 — CUE's first reading against the cue binary: the extractor declared a field only as a direct struct value within six levels and called only #definitions (L1 sym_recall .1278, L2 call_recall .1751)

- **Symptom:** the tolerated CUE extractor read L1 unmet (sym_recall .1278, 2,812 of 22,005 definitions) and L2 unmet (call_recall .1751, 160 of 914 pairs) on its first reading.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect cue -tool-oracle <cue-oracle output>` on cue-lang/cue v0.16.1 internal/ci, monogon/NetMeta b93a56d1 and fathom's schema/ at 90f0a01f, the oracle `cue lsp` behind a `cue vet` gate (calibration/oracles/cue, cue v0.16.1, tier A), fathom 90f0a01f.
  - **Expected:** every field the binary's language server lists as a document symbol is an entity, and every identifier it resolves to a field or definition is a reference of its innermost field.
  - **Actual:** missed fields sat in a conjunction's struct (`web: #Kit & {name: ...}`), a list element or element type, a comprehension's body or source, a let's value, a parenthesized selector base (`(#A & {in: x}).out`), and past depth six (NetMeta's CRDs imported into CUE: 16,863 of the 22,005 definitions in three files); missed references were every reference to a regular field, hidden field or let-reached field, every selector beyond an import's first, and references through value aliases (`#QueryVariable: V={... definition: V.query}`).
  - **Repro:** the commands in calibration/oracles/cue/README.md against the extractor at 90f0a01f.
- **Root cause:** the extractor recursed only into a field's direct StructLit value with a depth cap of six, and recorded only `#`-prefixed identifiers as calls (VS-3's original scope).
- **Fix:** LB-F212 (every struct literal anywhere in a value, cap 256, the language server's label-path naming), LB-F213 (every reference to a field or definition, resolved through the parser's binding and the file's own structs). The reading: sym_recall 1, sym_precision 1, call_recall 1, call_precision .9979.
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** CUE reads L1 and L2 excellent; product callers of a CUE field now include every field that reads it, not only those naming a definition.
