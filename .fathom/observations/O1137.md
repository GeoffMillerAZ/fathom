### O1137 — `gen-plan-cue` loses data on 49 of 75 active plans: ADR-018 decision 3's "lossless round-trip" is false and no gate checks it

- **Symptom:** `fathom gen-plan-cue --plan <plan> --service fathom` exits 2 with "round-trip loss — emitted CUE does not rebuild the parsed plan" for 49 of the 75 top-level plans on main (2026-10-17); the new `docs/plans/intent-v2.md` is the 50th of 76 (first divergence at a `[-]` row carrying `needs:` and `trigger:`).
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom gen-plan-cue` (`renderPlanCUE`, `planFromCUE`, `cmd/fathom/genplancue.go`; `schema/plan/schema.cue`)
  - **Expected:** ADR-018 decision 3: the generated `#Plan` document proves a lossless round trip, and the compatibility surface lists plan grammar v2 with `schema/plan/schema.cue` as evidence.
  - **Actual:** keys the markdown parser reads (among them `needs`, `certified_by`, `strength`, the `oracle: ledger` keys, `accept`, `retires`) are not carried through the CUE form, so the round trip diverges; `TestGenPlanCue_RoundTrip` covers a fixture, never the repository's plans, so the drift grew unseen.
  - **Repro:** `for p in docs/plans/*.md; do fathom gen-plan-cue --plan $p --service fathom >/dev/null 2>err || grep -q "round-trip loss" err && echo $p; done` (README excluded) lists 49 plans at `c6226ce1`.
- **Root cause:** keys were added to the grammar (G2, ADR-043, ADR-044, ADR-049) without the schema and the renderer, and no gate runs the round trip over real plans.
- **Fix (proposed shape):** complete `schema/plan` and the renderer, then a gate test round-trips every active plan. `docs/plans/intent-v2.md` IV-6, ADR-074 decision 5; the dated line under ADR-018.
- **Status:** fixed (IV-6, commit ebf07bb5): `schema/plan` declares every key the parser reads and `TestGenPlanCue_RoundTripsEveryActivePlan` gates the round trip; 0 of 76 active plans lose data (50 of 76 at base)
