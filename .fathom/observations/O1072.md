### O1072 — review_diff's findings bridge is stripped on every daemon: the auto-refresh wrapper forwards none of PutClaims, SetClaimStatus, SourceFingerprint, SetClaimValidTo

- **Symptom:** found beside O1068 (not reproduced end to end): review_diff type-asserts reviewClaimsBackend (PutClaims, ClaimsForService, SetClaimStatus, SourceFingerprint, SetClaimValidTo) on its backend; autoRefreshBackend, which every serve-http daemon with auto-refresh on (the default) wraps the client in, forwards only ClaimsForService, so the assertion fails and the daemon reports findings_persisted:false where an unwrapped client persists them.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** MCP review_diff through serve-http
  - **Expected:** review findings persisted as claims, as through the unwrapped client
  - **Actual:** (by reading) the capability is absent through the wrapper
  - **Repro:** `grep -n "func (a \*autoRefreshBackend) PutClaims" internal/mcp/*.go` finds nothing; review_tool.go reviewClaimsBackend
- **Root cause:** the O7 forwarding class: a capability added to the client and asserted by a tool, never forwarded by the wrapper.
