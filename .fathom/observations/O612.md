### O612 — an enum constant is no Java definition: 169 of guava's 212 unmapped index definitions

- **Symptom:** typed_recall .9515 and def_mapping .9701 (guava .9874) stop short of 1 by the index's enum constants
- **Evidence:** scratch classification of the unmapped definitions by descriptor (O609): guava 169 terms such as `StandardSystemProperty#JAVA_VERSION.`; the JavaParser dump's own `enumConstant` is excluded from L1's kind intersection (176 on guava, 4 on retrofit)
- **Root cause:** `dialects/java/tags.scm` defines no enum constant; the manifest maps ctags' and the tool's enumConstant to `enum_member` but declares no such kind
- **Fix:** none here: a real `JavaEnumConstant` definition would raise def_mapping and typed_recall and add one kind to L1's intersection (sym_precision against the dump moves with it), a dialect change with its goldens, not needed for L4 excellent
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** Java's L4 ceiling is .9515 until then
