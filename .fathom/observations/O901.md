- **Symptom:** the TSX holdout pinned with owner ruling M2 (5cdd77ca), read once at 61967bd3, reads level_met L3: L1, L2 and L3 excellent, L4 unmet. Ruling M2 settled L2 (O860): call_precision .934 and call_recall .9516 with 3,886 JSX element references counted as calls. LB-Q-typescript stays open.
- **Evidence:**

- **Root cause:** two classes outside every rule: TypeScript's module-level `let`/`var` is no definition in the dialect (LB-F382 names a const; ruling M1, LB-F470, made JavaScript's let and var definitions only), and a member of an anonymous type literal is a definition of the index under a `typeLiteral<n>:` meta descriptor (D2's rule leaves out object-literal properties, `<name><n>:`, not type-literal members) that no extractor names; a type alias's own literal members are named (`MenuRenderPropArg.open`), a nested one is not.
