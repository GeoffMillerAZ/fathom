### O1285 — `go:embed` of the fauxtown fixture silently drops polyglot-service

- **Symptom:** `fathom init --demo` copies 9 of the fixture fleet's 10 services; the embedded tree has no `polyglot-service`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `internal/connector/fauxtown_fs.go` (`//go:embed testdata/fauxtown`)
  - **Expected:** every service under `internal/connector/testdata/fauxtown`
  - **Actual:** the demo's first line reads `(9 services)`; `polyglot-service/go.mod` is the only go.mod in the fixture
  - **Repro:** `fathom init --demo`, or walk `connector.Fauxtown()` and list its top directories
- **Root cause:** embed leaves out any directory holding a go.mod (a module boundary), with no error, and every dot-file (the planted `.claude/worktrees` tree too).
- **Fix:** none needed for the demo (it ingests checkout-service); recorded so nobody reads the demo copy as the whole fixture. Renaming the fixture's go.mod (for example `go.mod.fixture`) would bring it in but changes what the polyglot ingest tests read.
- **Status:** open
