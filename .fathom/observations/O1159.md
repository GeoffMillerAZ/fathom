### O1159 — strict verify's verdict does not move with load or call shape: 78 compared calls over the fx2 six read byte-identical

- **Symptom:** wave fx2's integrator ran `fathom verify --strict` over every plan on a fresh scratch ingest while other gates loaded the machine, and 6 of 76 plans exited 1 (efficiency-wave, language-breadth, observatory-console, portable-receipts, retire-unused-analyzers, surface-reckoning); re-run one plan at a time on the same store, all 6 exited 0, and the release gate's single all-plans call passed. Suspected: a verdict that depends on load or call shape.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom verify --strict --json --store <scratch> --service fathom --ledger <scratch ledger>`, fathom built at 02673c14 from a clean clone, over one fresh scratch ingest of 02673c14 (9 m 18 s wall, typed_go 496 s wall / 29 s CPU on a machine at load 6-8).
  - **Expected:** if load or call shape mattered, some row's status or criteria would differ between shapes.
  - **Actual:** zero flips. (a) one call per plan, the 6 plans x 3 tries x 3 `--since` variants (none; `origin/main` = HEAD; `a7d18c2f`, fx2's own base, 15 commits back) = 54 calls, plus 6 under load: all exit 0. (b) one call over all 76 plans (77 `.md` less README), 3 tries x 3 variants = 9 calls: exit 0, 27-28 s wall, 27 s user. (c) the same under 8 busy loops on 10 cores (N-2, load average 9.6-16.9), 9 calls: exit 0, 45-50 s wall, 42-43 s user. Every all-plans run's verdict vector (id, status, label, per-criterion status) hashed the same (dcd92f0dcc) and its whole `reports` JSON, details included, was byte-identical (4852671b9f); each of the six plans hashed the same in all 60 single calls and all 18 all-plans extracts. 2117 claimed, 2117 verified, every run; 7 earlier text-mode calls (6 single, 1 all-plans) also exited 0.
  - **Repro:** `w-fx3-verifyload/shape.sh` (one result line per call; `load.sh 8` wraps a shape in 8 busy loops and kills only those).
- **Root cause:** none in load or call shape: store-mode verify has no wall-clock budget on a row's evidence (the 30 s and 60 s deadlines are HTTP-mode only), and the gone evidence reads HEAD and cleanliness once per process. O1160 reproduces fx2's exact six by another route.
- **Fix:** none; no code changed.
- **Status:** not reproduced (wave fx3, verifyload, 2026-10-17).
- **Impact:** a strict verify that fails on a loaded machine is not evidence of a nondeterministic verdict; read the failing rows' criteria first (O1160).
