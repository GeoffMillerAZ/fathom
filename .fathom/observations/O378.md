### O378 — tree-sitter-meson v1.3.0 does not read `a*b` or a `?:` ternary, and the ERROR node that follows swallows the statements after it in a long file (harfbuzz loses 21 of 163 definitions)

- **Symptom:** harfbuzz 14.5.1 sym_recall .8712 (142 of 163) against Meson's own parser; mesonbuild/meson's 1,606 files read .9898 in aggregate
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect meson -tool-oracle <meson_oracle.py>` (tier A, `mesonbuild.mparser`), `indexbench lang-probe -dialect meson` on harfbuzz's meson.build chunks
  - **Expected:** every `dependency()` assignment of the root file is a definition
  - **Actual:** the root file's 19 `x_dep = dependency(...)` assignments after `hb_version_int = 60000 + hb_version_major*100 + ...` (line 27) and the `?:` at line 121 are not extracted; the same lines parsed alone extract
  - **Repro:** `printf "x = executable('a','a.c')\nv = 6 + a*100\ny = executable('b','b.c')\n"` reads three ERROR nodes, and harfbuzz's root meson.build (lines 27 and 121) loses the assignments after them
- **Root cause:** the grammar has no multiplication or ternary rule; tree-sitter's recovery after the ERROR can absorb following statements
- **Fix:** none here; a grammar change upstream or a different grammar (master is 24 dependency-bump commits ahead of v1.3.0, no grammar change)
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** Meson L1 is excellent only in aggregate; a real project with arithmetic loses definitions after it
