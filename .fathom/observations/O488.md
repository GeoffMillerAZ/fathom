### O488 — `#requires-text!` guards lose on a big query: each distinct pruned subset compiles a variant of it (Gradle: 16.7 G to 95.4 G)

- **Symptom:** LB-M5's named candidates for a guard were gradle, cmake, commonlisp, helm, meson, conan and ruby; the first tried was Gradle.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** 73 `(#requires-text! ...)` lines added to gradle's tags.scm (53) and imports.scm (20) (the word every pattern's `#eq?` names: `include`, `plugins`, `apply`, `dependencies`, `project`, `task`, `tasks`, `dependsOn|finalizedBy`), ingest of the set's 31 `.gradle` files (okhttp, retrofit and the others) before and after, `TestGradleGuardsAreNecessary`-shaped comparison green over the testdata, 31 real scripts and 16 synthetic ones.
  - **Actual:** 16.7 G instructions guarded-off and 95.4 G guarded-on, the symbols identical (92). `Query.guarded` compiles a variant of the query for each distinct set of patterns a source keeps (up to maxQueryVariants, 16); an 800-line query costs about 5 G to compile, and 31 scripts used up the 16 variants.
  - **Not tried:** ruby's seven predicated patterns are its cheap ones (the heavy ones are every call and constant), cmake's 23 and the rest were not in the set's hot files.
- **Root cause:** a guard prunes per source but pays per distinct pruned subset; the HTML guards (four patterns, three variants over 536 pages) win, a query of 40 patterns and 7 independent words does not.
- **Fix:** none; the change was reverted. A guard belongs on a small query or a pattern family whose words co-occur, and wants a variant count before it lands (`Query.LastRunPatterns` over the corpus).
- **Status:** closed; the guard candidates of LB-M5 other than html stay unproven by measurement.
- **Impact:** negative result; saves the next worker a 5x regression on Gradle-heavy trees.
