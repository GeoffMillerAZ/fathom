### O459 — the PHP dispatch switch raises ingest cost per byte: guzzle 1.82× its instructions, the polyglot tree +2.0%, more than guzzle's own share

- **Symptom:** after `.php` moved from the CGO phpextract.go to the PHP tags dialect (LB-F183), the LB-M5 polyglot tree costs 501.5 G instructions and 46.55 s CPU against main's 491.7 G and 40.30 s (+2.0%, +15.5% CPU under load 13 to 21), and guzzle/guzzle ingested alone 9.1 G and 0.83 s against 5.0 G and 0.54 s
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom ingest --json` under `nice -n 10 /usr/bin/time -l`, the one-parse.md command sequence (footprint.md, "LB-M5 ... 2026-10-02 (wave ld, item dispatch)"), builds main 5c891a06 and head c34559d4, a fresh store, `FATHOM_HOME` and `FATHOM_CACHE_ROOT` per run
  - **Expected:** a switch that keeps one parse per file costs about what the incumbent's native parse cost
  - **Actual:** guzzle (70 PHP files, 745,912 bytes): `extract` 0.34 to 0.65 s CPU, peak RSS 67 to 143 MB, symbols 1,408 to 1,478, file-resolved edges 266 to 1,281; the whole tree +9.8 G instructions (+8.6 G between adjacent runs), `extract` 31.6 to 35.8 s and `store_writes` 7.5 to 8.9 s CPU between adjacent runs, more than the +4.1 G guzzle shows alone
  - **Repro:** the runs in footprint.md's wave ld section (guzzle_guzzle r2 at main and head; poly w4 at main and w8 at head)
- **Root cause:** partly understood: the parse moved from native C to the wasm engine (a one-time compile of the PHP grammar module in a CLI ingest, which compiles grammars in memory; the managed daemon's on-disk compilation cache amortizes it) and the dialect's output is larger (a signature and end line on every definition, a `PhpNamespace` per file, 1,015 more file-resolved edges through `use`d `Foo::bar` calls). Why the whole tree pays about 5.7 G more than guzzle alone (module-pool contention among the grammars a polyglot ingest keeps warm is the candidate) was not isolated
- **Fix:** none here (D1 does not gate on cost; the engine and pool are wave le's): profile a whole-tree ingest at main and head (`cmdIngest` under pprof) to place the extra 5.7 G, and read the tsengine pool's instantiation count with and without `.php` in the set
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** LB-M5 reads 2.26× CPU per byte on the branch (main 1.96×, both FAIL against 1.5×); the switch's share is small in instructions but the cost per PHP byte roughly doubles for a PHP-only repository
