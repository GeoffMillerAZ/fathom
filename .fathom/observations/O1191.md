### O1191 — RO-38's "own 1 s descriptor" contradicts I53, so the SubagentStop entry is 2 s with a 1 s budget

- **Symptom:** RO-38's row text puts `stop-hook --event subagent-stop` under "its own 1 s descriptor". I53 says no hook invocation outlives its descriptor's `TimeoutSec` minus max(1 s, 20%), and `TestNoHookRPCOutlivesItsDescriptorBudget` failed the 1 s row: `budget 500ms leaves 500ms of 1s`.
- **Evidence:** `internal/hookguard/deadline.go` `HookDeadline` (a 1 s timeout has no budget left after the 1 s margin, so it falls back to half), `cmd/fathom/hookguard_test.go` (the margin assertion), docs/intent/invariants.md I53.
- **Root cause:** the row sized the descriptor to the check (200 ms) and forgot the margin I53 reserves for the hook to exit.
- **Fix:** the SubagentStop binding is `TimeoutSec: 2` (internal/harness/descriptors.go), the least I53 allows, which leaves a 1 s budget; the check itself still runs inside its 200 ms (`claimCheckBudget`). A subagent's end still pays about 200 ms, never Stop's 7 s, which is what the row's 1 s protects.
- **Status:** fixed
- **Impact:** RO-38's text reads "1 s descriptor" where the code has a 2 s descriptor with a 1 s budget; the next plan edit can say so.
