### O387 — a Kyverno Pod policy governs Pod controllers through autogen, which the policy_ref join does not model

- **Symptom:** `kyverno test` evaluates a Pod rule (or a CEL policy whose resourceRules name pods) against Deployments, StatefulSets, DaemonSets, Jobs and CronJobs; the policy_ref edge joins only Pods
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `kyverno-oracle -kyverno /opt/homebrew/bin/kyverno` (kyverno test 1.18.1) on kyverno/policies main and release-1.18
  - **Expected:** a change to a Deployment's pod template reaches the Pod policy that Kyverno applies to it
  - **Actual:** 744 (main) and 2,259 (release-1.18) evaluated rows are autogen (an `autogen-` rule, or a controller row of a Pod policy) and have no edge; the cross-check counts them apart
  - **Repro:** the commands in calibration/oracles/kyverno/README.md
- **Root cause:** the dialect reads the kinds a policy writes; autogen is engine behaviour (default controllers, disabled by `pod-policies.kyverno.io/autogen-controllers: none` or a CEL `spec.autogen`, skipped for rules with names or selectors)
- **Fix:** none this wave; LB-X-kyverno-autogen (deferred) carries it
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** blast_radius from a Deployment misses the Pod policies that govern it through autogen
