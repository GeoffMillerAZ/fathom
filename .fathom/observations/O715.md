### O715 — a day with a deploy or a dark hour cannot read PASS under RT-M1

- **Symptom:** the verdict is INCOMPLETE for a day with fewer than 1,296 of 1,440 minute samples, and for a day the lifecycle ledger does not reach back to
- **Evidence:** `tenancy.JudgeRTM1`; the day log counts one sample per distinct UTC minute the sampler ticked, so a daemon stopped for `task deploy` loses those minutes; unclean exits and over-budget exits are read from `lifecycle.jsonl` (+ `.1`, two generations of 1 MiB), not from the sampler, which cannot see an unclean exit
- **Root cause:** deliberate: a mostly-dark day is not evidence of a bounded footprint, and a column the instrument could not see is unknown, never zero (I38)
- **Fix:** none; the 90% threshold (`tenancy.DayCompleteSamples`) is the knob if a real reading shows deploy days routinely tipping it. Violations always FAIL regardless of coverage
- **Status:** open (revisit with the first real seven-day reading)
- **Impact:** the operator reading RT-M1; a deploy mid-week may push the reading a day later
