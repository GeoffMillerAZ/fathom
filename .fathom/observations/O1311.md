### O1311 — a tooling dialect's `calls` in a tool oracle are counted and never graded

- **Symptom:** a `kind: tooling` dialect measured with a tool oracle whose pairs are under `calls` reads `level_met L1` and `L2 not-attempted`, though the reading's counts carry `call_truth`, `call_truth_found` and `call_fathom`.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle` / `langbench.merge` (`internal/langbench/merge.go`, the `r.Kind == "tooling"` key list)
  - **Expected:** an oracle's call pairs grade L2 for any dialect, or the harness refuses them for a tooling one
  - **Actual:** a tooling dialect's L2 metrics are `ref_recall` and `ref_precision`; its `calls` family is scored into counts and no metric reads it, with no warning
  - **Repro:** jupyter, 2026-10-19: the same pairs under `calls` gave `L2 not-attempted` with 3,781 call pairs counted; under `references` they gave `L2 good`
- **Root cause:** `Kind == "tooling"` keys the metric list on sym, ref and xref only.
- **Fix:** the jupyter oracle writes `references`. Not fixed in the harness (not this item's file).
- **Status:** open
