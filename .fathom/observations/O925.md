### O925 — HTML's L3 reading: one page-linking subject turns "not measured" into L3 excellent, on a denominator of 178 links

- **Symptom:** html.json read L2 with "L3 not met: import_resolution not measured" (O841) because neither committed subject linked a page to another.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect html` at 90c7b068 on bootstrap (`js/tests/visual`), html5-boilerplate v9.0.1 and StartBootstrap/startbootstrap-sb-admin-2 v4.1.4 (f0309881, MIT, committed alone first as LB-F506), parse5 8.0.1 and lychee 0.24.2 `--offline`; read once.
  - **Actual:** level_met L3, L1 to L3 excellent. sb-admin-2: 14 pages, 552 local links lychee resolves, 178 between measured pages (import_resolution 1, 178 of 178; file_edge_precision and file_edge_recall 1, 151 of 151); 75 links to scripts, stylesheets and images are out of scope (import_oracle_out_of_scope); 150 (document, id) pairs, call_recall and call_precision 1, 2 duplicate ids leave 4 references out of both sides, 57 references name no id of their document and stay in the truth and are all found.
- **Root cause:** the queue chose its subjects for ids and forms before HTML had an L3 oracle (O841); a page-to-page link denominator needs a multi-page site.
- **Fix:** the subject, not a code change (no extraction change, no tuning after the reading). AdminLTE v4.9.1 (MIT) was vetted and left: a 100-page built dist/ tree where sb-admin-2 is 14 hand-written pages.
- **Status:** closed (LB-F507)
- **Impact:** LB-Q-html can flip on html.json itself; the spent holdout's reading is not reused. The single page-linking subject is one site's template, so the L3 grade rests on a narrow sample.
