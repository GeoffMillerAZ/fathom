### O384 — no parser in scope reads Cedar under the 5% line, and the reference CLI's JSON forms carry no source positions

- **Symptom:** LB-Q-cedar refused at pin (LB-Q-cedar-rest)
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** tree-sitter CLI 0.27.0 on chrnorm/tree-sitter-cedar 5092204d; a scratch program on cedar-go v1.8.0; cedar-policy-cli 4.13.0 check-parse on cedar-policy/cedar-examples c251f7d1 and cedar-integration-tests v4.13.0
  - **Expected:** a parser reading the 42 policy and 17 schema files the CLI accepts with at most 5% in error, with lines
  - **Actual:** tree-sitter-cedar errors on 5 of 42 policy files (namespaced action entities, a string-key index, a multi-line annotation) and has no schema grammar; cedar-go rejects 6 of 42 (templates) and its schema AST has no positions; `translate-policy` and `translate-schema` JSON have no positions either, so the oracle locates lines itself
  - **Repro:** calibration/oracles/cedar/README.md
- **Root cause:** no allowlisted grammar or library covers Cedar's policies and schemas with positions
- **Fix:** none here; the trigger is a native reader under ADR-048's wave-pc admission (owner's call) or a grammar or library that passes
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** .cedar and .cedarschema stay disclosed as unsupported; the subjects and the tier-A oracle are committed for the re-plan
