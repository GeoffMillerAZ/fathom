### O1238 — K5 cannot be read from `fathom resources --days`: rung entries are counted, shed minutes are not split by the trigger that entered them

- **Symptom:** responsible-tenant §G's kill criterion K5 reads "RT-M13 FAILs with half or more of its shed minutes entered at combined ≥ allowance". RT-189's day rows count rung entries by rung, trigger and the pressure at entry, and keep one `shed_minutes` total. They do not say how many shed minutes followed each entry.
- **Evidence:** wave ten2 (tenancy), commit 675320b3. `DaySummary.RungEntries` is a count per (rung, trigger, pressure), and `ShedMinutes` is one total. Since RT-192, a `pressure` shed entry implies combined ≥ the allowance at entry (`hostPressureApplies` gates it). So entry counts give K5's direction but not its minute share.
- **Root cause:** the row asked for entries with the pressure at entry, and K5 asks for minutes. The day log rolls minutes per level, not per entering trigger.
- **Fix:** if RT-M13 FAILs, read K5 from the ring's transitions for the days the ring still holds (24 h), or add an additive `shed_minutes_by_trigger` to the day row, counted against the trigger that entered the current rung, before the next window opens. Do not estimate the share from entry counts.
- **Status:** resolved (2026-10-18): day rows carry `shed_minutes_by_trigger`, rolled on the sampler tick (a rise sets the trigger of every rung it passes; a step-down from refuse to shed returns to the trigger that entered shed), summing to shed + refuse minutes; `rtm13` carries the week's split with pre-split minutes named `unattributed_shed_minutes`, and a FAIL line prints the pressure share (`TestDaySummaryAttributesShedMinutesToTheEnteringTrigger`). Pressure-keyed refuse minutes entered at `critical` are not gated by the allowance, so on a refuse-heavy FAIL the pressure share bounds K5 from above.
- **Impact:** a K5 reading after an RT-M13 FAIL needs the ring or a follow-up field. The product's day log alone cannot settle it.
