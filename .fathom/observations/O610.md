### O610 — `.java` through the wasm dialect costs 1.58x the instructions of the CGO walker on guava, 1.05x on retrofit

- **Symptom:** dispatching `.java` to the tags dialect (LB-F302) raises the ingest's cost
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `/usr/bin/time -l fathom ingest` into a scratch store, the CGO incumbent build against the dialect build, same machine, run once each (wave lf1, O515 precedent)
  - **Actual:** guava's module directory (611 files): 30.4 to 48.1 billion instructions retired (1.58x), wall 2.99 to 3.20 s, extract 1.73 to 2.56 s, peak footprint 175 to 206 MB, file-resolved edges 3,547 to 9,847, symbols 22,882 to 23,851; retrofit (the whole checkout): 87.6 to 92.2 billion (1.05x), wall 4.82 to 5.12 s, 426 to 446 MB, file-resolved edges 1,458 to 3,464, symbols 15,297 to 16,050
  - **Repro:** build `./cmd/fathom` at the commit before and after the register.go switch; `fathom ingest -service s -path <subject> -store <scratch>.sqlite`
- **Root cause:** the wasm parse replaces the native one, and the dialect now writes a file's imports and references for the resolver (far more file-resolved edges)
- **Fix:** none; the cost is the price of the dialect's recall (call_recall .8408 to .9529, file_edge_recall .2922 to .829)
- **Status:** open (disclosed)
- **Impact:** a Java-heavy repository ingests about 7% slower in wall time on these two subjects
