### O1399 — RO-28 lifts the hook's as-matched figures from 0.115% to 1.015% of Bash bytes; the projection still reads three times that

- **Symptom:** RO-M0 (`docs/bench/readings/readouts/corpus-replay.md` finding 4) read the hook as seeing almost nothing: every rule together would remove 0.09% of Bash bytes as matched against 3.11% projected, because the calls are compound. RO-28 now classifies a call past its leading `&&`-joined quiet steps, so the question was how much of the projection the hook reaches.
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `fathom readout replay --days 31 --json`, run twice on 2026-10-19 over the same local transcripts (154,726 Bash calls, 409.6 MB, 2026-09-19 to 2026-10-20; 31 days so the window covers RO-M0's 30 from 2026-10-18; no model): the binary at origin/main (ab4cab36b) and the one at ro4-quiet 5a23c20cd. Built-ins only (no user rule files and no `[readouts]` table on the machine). Aggregates only, not committed.
  - **Tool:** per class, as matched before → after RO-28 (would-remove share of Bash bytes; hits), against the projection (unchanged by RO-28):

    | Rule | Before | After | Projected |
    |---|---:|---:|---:|
    | grep-group@1 | 0.004% (388) | 0.031% (4,414) | 0.546% (31,948) |
    | read-outline@1 | 0.104% (174) | 0.791% (778) | 2.182% (3,569) |
    | git-diff@1 | 0.001% (12) | 0.150% (588) | 0.231% (2,134) |

    Eligible for lean, as matched: none before, grep-group@1 after (0.031%); projected: grep-group@1 and git-grep-group@1 (0.562%). no-rule calls fell from 93,777 to 88,059.
  - **Tool:** the replay's effective shapes (each call past the projection's quiet segments, which also strips `;` and newline joins): of 38,018 effective `grep` calls, 25,495 are still a list and 9,652 a pipeline; `go test` 4,825 lists and 2,051 pipelines of 6,885; `sed -n` 9,368 lists of 18,141.
- **Root cause:** the hook now reaches 32% of the projected bytes (1.015 of 3.146 points). The rest is what RO-28's contract declines by design: a leading segment that is not quiet (`echo ---; grep ...`, `make && go test`), a `;` or newline join, a pipe into something other than a display tail, a redirect. The projection reads each of those as its last segment over the call's whole output, so it overstates what any per-command rule can see. The largest share by calls is lists with a non-quiet leading segment. This apportioning is by calls from the effective-shape table: bytes per cause were not measured.
- **Fix:** none needed for RO-28. The 3.11% projection that RO-M0's CUT verdict read is an upper bound; at 1.015% as matched, the CUT stands with more margin. RO-M1's bar ("each class's would-remove share within 2x of RO-M0's") compares the hook with RO-M0's projected figures, and as matched grep-group (0.031% against 0.535%) and go-test (0.006% against 0.101%) sit far outside 2x. When RO-M1 is read, the comparison should be against the replay's as-matched figures, not the projection, or the bar fails on the projection's overstatement rather than on the hook.
- **Status:** open (RO-M1's comparison basis is the owner's call)
