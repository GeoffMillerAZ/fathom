### O659 — the push gate on the internal disk failed under swap pressure: the stranger daemon refused to ingest

- **Symptom:** v0.10.0 gate run 1 failed in stranger-check (`fathom init` timeout) while swap was about 19 GB and the internal disk had under 5 GiB free (REL RESULT (c), 2026-10-09)
- **Evidence:** the throwaway daemon's tenancy ladder refuses below `min_swap_volume_free` (5 GiB) on the swap volume; runs 2 and 3 with the gate dir on the external volume (min 6 GiB free) under the TCC keeper were green with no EPERM. Same class as O220
- **Root cause:** the gate's work dir and caches lived on the internal disk, which is also the swap volume, so parallel gates and swap shared one small free-space budget
- **Fix:** decided (GATE MOVE, owner-architect, 2026-10-09 15:45): `push-internal-disk.sh` runs the gate on the external volume by default (a `gate-ext` directory under the build scratch tree, its own TCC keeper, internal floor 5 GiB, gate volume floor 15 GiB, cache cap 12 GB; `FATHOM_GATE_DIR` restores the internal path); the internal-disk gate directory removed, internal free 6.5 to 19 GiB
- **Status:** closed (2026-10-09, GATE MOVE)
- **Impact:** every push gate on this machine while waves ran in parallel; the product gap that hid the cause (O220: stranger-check drops init's `MANUAL: ingest` line) is still open
