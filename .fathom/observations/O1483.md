### O1483 — Conservation regressions have no input: `fathom conserve` persists no reading

- **Symptom:** the /progress regressions list shows certificate and invariant-enforcement regressions but never a conservation plane regression.
- **Evidence:** `conservationRegressions` takes `[]ConservationReading`; no ledger or store table records a plane's verdict per commit, so `AllProofRegressions` is called with none.
- **Root cause:** `fathom conserve` prints its report at every push and stores nothing, and the table freeze (RT-M1) bars a new capture.
- **Fix:** after RT-M1 reads PASS, append each push's per-plane verdict to an existing ledger and feed it to the page. The empty-list text names this gap.
- **Status:** open
- **Impact:** a plane that stops being `conserved` is not listed; invariant enforcement regressions are, through the certificate runs.
