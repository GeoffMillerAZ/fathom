### O696 — the express-regex reader is retired: what it read and what goes unread now

- **Symptom:** the legacy line regex (`legacy_express.go`, the only JS/TS route reader before wave c2) read any `x.get|post|…|use('/path'` in any JS/TS file as a route
- **Evidence:** on the wave c2 Node corpora (`docs/bench/readings/routes/node.json`, `before_express_regex`): Express examples route recall .6974, precision .8154 (USE rows for `app.use`, routers at their unmounted local paths), bound recall 0 (it captured `user.list` or `function` as the handler); Nest 0 of 78; Fastify 3 of 26 at precision .20 (autoload plugins at their local paths); Next.js 1 of 21 at precision .125, including `GET /@undefinedImage.all` read from client code in the OpenTelemetry frontend and six invented routes in next-learn (7 read, 1 right); fauxtown unchanged (no JS/TS route either way)
- **Root cause:** a line regex has no receiver, no mount and no comment or string awareness
- **Fix:** deleted with LB-F328; the Express, Fastify, Nest and Next.js readers replace it (bound recall and precision 1.0 on Nest, Fastify and Next.js, .7632 / 1.0 on Express). `TestExtractJS` now expects the router's composed path and `TestLegacyReadersRegistered` the python-regex reader alone. What the regex read and no reader reads now: Koa (`@koa/router`), Hono, restify and hapi routes, and `app.METHOD` on a receiver named other than app or router in a file that never imports express. Trigger: a corpus with one of those frameworks (a reader per framework, measured like these)
- **Status:** fixed (retired); the unread frameworks are open
- **Impact:** fewer invented routes and USE rows in every JS/TS service; Koa, Hono and restify services lose the (unbound) routes the regex happened to read
