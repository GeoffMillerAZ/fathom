### O407 — doc-engine `where` filters the key an entity was reached by, never the keys it holds; dotted keys have no selector

- **Symptom:** an Ansible play and a task file's item cannot be told apart, and `ansible.builtin.import_tasks` cannot be selected
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** internal/extractor/docengine.go whereMatches and parseDocPath (docKeyRe `^[^.\[\]|*\s]+`)
  - **Expected:** `where: {not_key_in: [hosts]}` excludes an item that has a hosts key
  - **Actual:** `key_in`, `not_key_in`, `key_prefix` and `key_not_prefix` compare the key the node was reached by (empty for a sequence item); only `has_key` reads the node's own keys, and there is no negative `has_key`. A key containing a dot cannot be written in a selector
  - **Repro:** two entities at `$[*]`, one `has_key: [hosts]` and one without a filter: a named play is emitted twice
- **Root cause:** the §4 subset has no negation and no quoted key (a deliberate non-language)
- **Fix:** ansible reads a play as a task (one kind, signature = hosts) and selects every include and role key as `TL.*` with `where.key_in`, FQCN forms listed; a negative `has_key` and a quoted-key step are appfw's or the next doc-engine item's
- **Status:** worked around
- **Impact:** Ansible's L1 has no separate play kind and no "else module" naming (a task without a name is no entity)
