### O593 — Python's member pass (LB-F241) would drop C# members the oracles count: an `#if`/`#else` re-declaration and every indexer overload after the first

- **Symptom:** with the member pass extracting C# fields, events and indexers as Python's attributes are, L1 lost 11 definitions on Polly and Newtonsoft.Json against the Roslyn syntax dump
- **Evidence:**
  - **Tool:** `indexbench lang-oracle -dialect csharp -tool-oracle <dump defs> -unmatched` (wave lf1)
  - **Expected:** each declarator a definition, as the dump and scip-dotnet write them
  - **Actual:** the pass keeps one member per owner and name: a field declared in both branches of `#if DEBUG`/`#else` and every `this[..]` overload after the first are one definition
  - **Repro:** the member pass (`@member` captures, tsextract_members.go) on `dialects/csharp/testdata/members.cs` with an indexer overload
- **Root cause:** Python's rule (an attribute assigned twice is one attribute) is not C#'s (a declaration is a declaration)
- **Fix:** the C# dialect names its members through the manifest's container flag on the member kinds, which qualifies them Owner.name without the dedupe (LB-F289)
- **Status:** closed (the pass is not used for C#)
- **Impact:** sym_precision .9958 to .9985 and def_mapping .8222 to .9984 with every declarator kept
